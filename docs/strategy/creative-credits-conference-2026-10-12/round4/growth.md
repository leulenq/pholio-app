## Exactly how a credit enters the record

[BET] For a retrospective FWB pilot, an authorized organizer supplies a minimal per-show roster privately, distinguishing applicants, selected people and actual completed roles. Application and acceptance are not credits. A casting coordinator or backstage department head identifies completion evidence—final running order reconciled with check-in, substitutions and their firsthand knowledge. A named person with documented authority to attest for the organization signs specific role/date/show assertions; an ID check alone cannot establish that authority.

[BET] Uncertain records remain pending. Crew departments may need different signers because the casting coordinator cannot attest who did makeup. No universal bulk approval simply because names appear in a spreadsheet. The organizer sends an optional invitation through a permitted existing channel, describing the limited credit-claim purpose. Pholio receives contact information only if transfer is authorized. The participant confirms identity linkage, role and public-name permission, with correction/removal available. Unclaimed names remain private; an account is optional for receiving the attribution export. Separate opt-in governs product marketing. Photos require separate publication permission; a credit grants no image rights.

[ASSUMPTION] This may cost more reconciliation than its benefit. Measure that cost before calling issuance automatic.
