# Round 3: Competition

## 1. Vote: AMEND
H is my own proposal, so I attack it hardest. As drafted, its wedge (photographer-led magazine editorials) is the slice Kavyar already owns.

## 2. Most fatal flaw: Kavyar kills H fastest, with one feature
Today Kavyar has almost every part of H except confirmation by the credited person. From its help-centre article index ([index](https://help.kavyar.com/category/l0kij2tfxn-for-publishers)):
- **Credits:**
  - Team credits are *required* on every photo, with an Instagram handle and autocomplete against existing profiles. Editing a credit "updates the named collaborator's Kavyar profile" ([FACT](https://help.kavyar.com/article/jzdbs3f6zg-how-to-add-team-credits-to-a-photo)).
- **Embargo:** submission exclusivity periods, and tear sheets released on a **delayed date** ([FACT](https://help.kavyar.com/article/2emntp7tip-adding-tearsheets-to-a-submission)), which is close to an issued, embargoed credit.
- **The confirm/deny pattern:** agency rosters already run claim-and-confirm states (Confirmed / Unconfirmed / Denied) ([FACT](https://help.kavyar.com/article/yskdyinuwq-what-are-agency-rosters)).
- **Paying creatives:** PRO at $14, ELITE at $32 ([FACT](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1510274904)).

**How the kill works [ASSUMPTION on timing]:**
- Kavyar points its roster-confirmation pattern at photo credits: "you were credited as Stylist on X, confirm or deny." It adds "saved teams" so a lead can draft a submission before the shoot.
- That is weeks of work on code it already has. The result is a confirmed production record that sits on the publication rail, among creatives who already pay.
- If our seed leads use Pholio mainly to pre-fill Kavyar, Pholio becomes Kavyar's free R&D lab. Our "Export to Kavyar" output also tells Kavyar exactly what to copy. I found no public Kavyar API [ASSUMPTION: none found], so the export is copy-paste at best.
- Runner-up: **Instagram** (roles and confirmation on collabs, no 6-account cap). Less likely, since enhanced tags are untouched since 2022, but more damaging.

**Why H can survive [BET]:** Kavyar sees only work that is submitted to a magazine. It structurally misses:
- runway (FWB);
- lookbooks for small designers;
- music videos;
- embargoed client and NDA work;
- tests that were never submitted.

Creative's round-1 point applies here: those are exactly the credits that are lost today. That is H's ground, and the draft's wedge points at the wrong slice.

## 5. Unresolved #2 (Kavyar): why type the roster into Pholio first?
- **For a magazine editorial, they won't, and H shouldn't ask them to.** Kavyar is the shorter path and the one that leads to publication.
- **For a lookbook, a runway show, a music video or an embargoed client job, there is no Kavyar step**, and the credit currently lives in DMs or nowhere. That is where the lead types the team into Pholio first. It is also where "Confirmed by" and the embargo vault do work nobody else does.
- An editorial can still enter Pholio *afterwards* (paste the team, members confirm), as a record, not the wedge.
- **Honest risk [ASSUMPTION]:** for a working NYC photographer, non-magazine productions may be a minority of output. That is unmeasured, and it is the test below.

## 6. The 14-day test with no code
Ask 15 NYC shoot leads (photographers and stylists) to list their **last 10 productions**. For each, record:
- the type;
- whether it went through Kavyar;
- where the team credits live now (Instagram caption, Kavyar, nowhere);
- whether anyone was left uncredited.

How the result moves my vote:
- **≥60% of productions went through Kavyar:** H is a Kavyar feature, and I vote REJECT. The founders should fall back to A as a small tool, or partner with Kavyar.
- **≥50% non-Kavyar, with credits missing or scattered:** I vote ACCEPT.
