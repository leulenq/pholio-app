# Round 5 — Devil’s advocate: stop the thesis, do not bless the replacement

**[BET] Vote: AMEND V2. Forced choice: STOP THESIS.** The universal verified-creative-work identity thesis has not earned another build. Optional factual attribution survives as ordinary portfolio content; no demonstrated proprietary network, reader advantage or paid business survives. V2 must specify that failing Mosaic is not a permission slip to build an organizer desk.

## Mosaic is a reason to refrain, not proof of demand

[FACT] Current [Mosaic help](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/mosaic-help) documents free credit creation, named company-domain approvers, specific roles and project descriptions, approval before publication, and up to five collaborators. It distinguishes paid guild verification from free authenticated credits. That covers the proposed assistant-initiated recognition workflow substantially enough to defeat novelty claims.

[FACT] Its [terms](https://mosaic.creatorsguildofamerica.org/terms) acknowledge limits: company email does not establish authority and authentication does not independently establish every detail. [ASSUMPTION] This is operational competition, not merely attractive marketing. But documented functionality is not independently observed completion, retention, reader utility or economic demand. Bulk FWB issuance and organizer intake remain unknown, not proven absent.

**[BET] Counterfactual:** if Mosaic offered free bulk FWB issuance tomorrow, **no**, Pholio would not deserve to exist for the credit thesis tested. A casting coordinator could still have a distinct paid reconciliation problem, but nothing yet demonstrates that problem exceeds Forms, Sheets and existing portfolios. Naming that possible payer does not restore the original thesis.

## Attack the latest consensus

**VC:** [ASSUMPTION] Your “portfolio and returning-talent desk” is the third coordinated retreat, after issuer desk and production record. Reusable records compound inside Google Sheets too. Until an independent buyer pays for an observed failure that existing tools cannot economically solve, the new slogan is an adjacent hypothesis—not the strongest truth.

**Competition:** [ASSUMPTION] Your “we should try [Mosaic] before rebuilding it” is reasonable only if someone actually needs an authenticated record. A smooth approval trial can validate a free publishing workflow nobody consequentially reads. Letting Mosaic do it removes engineering expense, not founder opportunity cost. Do not make onboarding credits our default next project.

**Hirer’s strongest attack on me:** [ASSUMPTION] You correctly say provenance changing a shortlist need not improve it; prestige may distort judgment. I accept that correction. Count verified useful reference information, avoided factual mistakes or total time saved, including approval effort. Do not count changed preferences, views or honorary attention as value.

## Why not stop altogether?

[BET] Stopping is the default, not a threat reserved for failed retention. The strongest alternative is **USE MOSAIC**, an uncommitted, user-requested service trial—not an endorsement that a credit market exists. That becomes justified only when a consenting creative identifies a current real recipient and a specific attribution question ordinary portfolio evidence leaves unanswered.

[BET] One bounded falsifier: across ten such live cases, compare existing evidence with Mosaic-assisted provenance; require three independently documented useful checks or net time savings before further founder investment. Include failed approvals and administrative cost. This threshold is a precommitted exploration decision, not a benchmark. If nobody supplies ten legitimate cases, or nobody uses the resulting evidence consequentially, stop. A five-collaborator cap alone never flips my vote.
