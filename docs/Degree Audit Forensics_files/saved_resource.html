Findings that change the plan

Ordered by how much they move the outcome, not by how the audit lists them. The first two invalidate your current draft outright.

Blocker

Four of your five remaining major-core courses have never been taught

I pulled the myUK Course Offering History for each. STA 415 and STA 425 show zero sections in every term back to 2009. STA 310 has run exactly once (Spring 2026) and STA 315 exactly once (Fall 2026). STA 495 shows no confirmed section either.

This is consistent with the major being new — the first cohort is only now reaching the 400 level — but it means the back half of your degree depends on courses whose schedule does not yet exist. You cannot plan around an offering pattern that has no history. The Director of Undergraduate Studies is the only source of truth here, and this is the single highest-value email you can send.

Blocker

Your draft violates prerequisites in two places

STA 415 requires MA 322, STA 310 and STA 315. Your draft has STA 315 and STA 415 in the same term (Fall 2027). STA 495 requires completion of all Major Core and Other Courses requirements — your draft has it alongside STA 425 in Spring 2028.

Useful corollary: STA 425 does not depend on STA 415. Both become legal after Fall 2027, so they can run in parallel rather than in series — that recovers one semester if the department offers them concurrently.

Gap

Your plan ends at 118 credits with four requirements untouched

Switching the audit to Completed, Registered, and Planned shows 96% progress and 118.00 credit hours — two short of 120. Four requirements remain unmet with everything you have planned already counted: A&S Foreign Language, Honors Elective (3), Honors Experiences (6), and Honors Thesis (3).

That is 12 Honors credits plus 0–14 language credits on top of the 118. Your realistic total is 130–142 credits, not 120. Spring 2028 graduation is not reachable from here on either the credit count or the STA sequence.

Verify with the director (Brent Harrison) whether the elective may double-count with your major.

Time-critical

Foreign language may cost 0 credits or 11 — and the cheap door has a September deadline

Your OTH 091 / OTH 092 high-school language credit satisfied the UK Core expectation but carries 0.00 hours and does not satisfy the separate A&S college requirement. Both college options are open and both are unmet.

UK's Modern & Classical Languages department runs a proficiency evaluation for languages not taught at UK — a BYU test ($50, 60+ languages) or a Penn Language Center test ($175). It grants equivalency, not credit hours, but equivalency is what the A&S requirement is checking. Tests run twice a year: the second Friday in October and the first Friday in March, and registration closes three weeks prior.

That puts the next deadline around 18 September 2026. Contact Liliana Drucker (lilo@uky.edu) and confirm with your A&S advisor first, since the policy is applied per college. If this route fails, the fallback is Option 2 — three levels of a new language, e.g. SPA 101 (4) + 102 (4) + 201 (3) = 11 credits.

Fragile

PSY 312 is your only viable psychology course, and it has a gate

Nearly every PSY course on the Cognitive Science elective list requires PSY 215/216 plus a declared Psychology major (PSY 427, 456, and the 56x seminars are effectively closed to you). PSY 312 — Brain and Behavior is the exception: its prerequisite reads "PSY 100 or equivalent and PSY 215 or PSY 216 or STA 296 and declared major or minor." Your STA 296 satisfies the middle clause.

Two risks remain: whether a Cognitive Science minor counts as the "declared minor," and that registration is restricted to psychology and neuroscience majors during the priority window. PSY 312 does triple duty for you, so confirm it early and hold a backup (see §3).

The one hard wall

"Students cannot use the same course to satisfy both UK Core and Arts and Sciences area requirements." Science and social science each cost two distinct courses — four courses, roughly 13 credits, that buy you nothing beyond breadth. The strategy is therefore to make each of those four carry a second job: an Honors slot (HON 152, HON 251), a minor elective (PSY 312), or the lab requirement (ANT 230, PSY 100).

Where overlap is not available

  • Honors Thesis (3 hrs) — Statistics has no departmental honors track, so HON 491 has no second job. Pure cost.
  • Foreign language (0 or 11 hrs) — language credits count toward the 120 and toward A&S 90, but nothing else. Either waive it or absorb it.
  • ICT 205 (3 hrs) — buys the AI certificate and counts toward the 120, but ICT is outside A&S, so it does not help the 90-hour or 60-hour requirements.
  • Honors Experiences (6 hrs) — the only way to make these productive is to route them through research that becomes your thesis, rather than treating them as a separate box.
