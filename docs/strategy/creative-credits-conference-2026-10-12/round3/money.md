# Round 3: money

## 1. Vote: AMEND

## 2. The fatal flaw: H charges the person who gets the least from the record

In H, the **lead** pays $12/mo to do the roster admin, and the credit mostly benefits the **members**.
- **The photographer is already credited by default.** Brand reposts credit "only the photographer, or nobody" [ASSUMPTION, creative R1]. Instagram enhanced tags favour the poster [FACT](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-enhanced-tags-creator-credits-announcement).
- **The people who gain most from a full-team record never lead.** Assistants, HMU, dressers and manicurists are the ones who gain. Founder R1 named "the uncredited assistant" as "our most motivated user". Creative R2 notes that models, assistants and MUAs "almost never organize".
- **The lead already pays a competitor.** Kavyar charges creatives $14 or $32 a month [FACT](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1510274904), and that is where the lead's credits already go.

So H's payer gets the least benefit from the record, does the most work, and is already paying for an alternative. Charging the lead to credit other people is a tax on generosity. [BET] Leads will use a free team sheet, but they will only pay for things that do *their own* job: the call sheet, release and consent capture, and the submission. Everything that only makes others visible has to stay free. If it doesn't, the lead simply leaves people off the sheet, which is the same failure Instagram has today.

## 3. Amendments (exact replacement text)

**Line 33.** Replace it with:
> Lead creatives pay about $12/mo only for tools that do the lead's own job: more than 3 active productions per month, call sheet, model-release and consent capture, and submission exports. Credit pages, the embargo vault, and any export of a person's own record are free for everyone. Pholio never sells an attestation or an "evidence pack".

**Line 34.** Replace it with:
> Organizer intake is free for vetted issuers. It is the trust anchor, and NYC's organizer market is worth under $100k ARR even if charged (money R2). The second paid line is agency intake workspaces, from month 9, at $49–99/mo on a card. They pay for filtering and snapshots of the applications they already receive, never for contact with creatives or for ranking.

**Line 36.** Append:
> Money gate: in a 30-day priced trial, at least 3% of claimed creatives who led 2 or more productions within 60 days convert to paid Pro. If the loop passes and conversion fails, Pholio is a network without a payer and raises no more than a seed.

## 4. Rebuttals of attacks on my position

**Trust: "Once Pholio sells a dossier for federal filings, our attestation becomes a material statement to USCIS. … Cut it."**
Conceded, and creative's "that is Flanelle's business model" is right too. The amendment to line 33 removes paid evidence packs entirely. I don't concede the principle, though. Charging for a production tool is not charging at the gate. Kavyar's $14 PRO buys submission workflow, not credibility.

**Devil: "Money's own Payer 3 depends on '≥200 distinct hirer accounts searching weekly.' That revives the search-seat idea…"**
Wrong reading. Payer 3 was a *conditional kill switch*. It stated that seats are never built unless hirer behaviour shows up first, and that condition is exactly the behaviour I said doesn't exist. My R2 removed it anyway ("Search seats stay dead"). Hirer is right that paid contact is the Snapcast pattern seen from the other side, so that is gone too.

## 5. Unresolved problem #5: the revenue ceiling, honestly

| Line | Arithmetic | ARR |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Pro, NYC | 2,000 × $144 [ASSUMPTION on the 2,000] | $288k |
| Lead Pro, $10M target | $10M ÷ $144 = 69k payers; at VC's 3% conversion that needs about 2.3M claimed creatives | $10M |

| Organizer intake | Free (worth under $100k anyway) | ~$0 |

My verdict:
- **On today's evidence, H is a good small business, roughly $1–3M ARR, if it spreads beyond NYC and beyond fashion.**
- It becomes venture-scale only if the Production becomes the default team record across disciplines (music video, editorial, events, film shorts) and pulls in millions of claimed creatives. Behance shows that scale is reachable (56M members [FACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behance)). Behance also shows that scale alone doesn't create a payer.

**Growth's question ("name the number that flips it"):**
- Fund B's loop over A whenever at least 15% of invitees open their own production within 60 days, even at zero revenue. A is capped below $100k in NYC, so any working loop is worth more than A.
- If the loop is under 10%, A is the business, and nobody should raise venture money.

## 6. One cheap 14-day test, with no code

**A concierge pre-sale.**
1. Pick 30 NYC photographers and stylists from Kavyar-published NYC editorials and the founders' network.
2. Offer each a $12/mo founding membership, paid now through a Stripe Payment Link. This is Pholio's own billing, with no money moving between other parties.
3. In return, the founders run that person's next two productions by hand: a call sheet, release and consent collection, a full-team credit page (a Google Doc or Notion page), a paste-ready credit block, and Kavyar-ready credits.

**How it changes my vote:**
- 5 or more paid out of 30 moves me to ACCEPT.
- 1 or fewer moves me to REJECT lead-pays. The money then moves entirely to agency intake, and H has to justify itself as a free network with no payer.
