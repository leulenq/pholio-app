# Round 1 — HIRER (brand producer / casting lead / music-video commissioner)

## 1. Verdict
The thesis builds a record I will never search, asks me to confirm credits my legal team won't let me confirm, and then plans to charge me for it. Of everything on the page, the only part I would use is the part that lowers my risk when I book someone, and the thesis treats that as a side effect.

## 2. My three strongest arguments

**A. I don't find people by searching credit databases, so "paid search seats" describes a buyer who doesn't exist.**
- [FACT] Commercial buyers mostly find photographers and directors through curated portfolio platforms (44%) and their own network (24%). Only 7% use Google or social search ([AtEdge 2024](https://campaigns.at-edge.com/survey-results/), R4 §1).
- [FACT] 82% of fashion, film and TV jobs go through contacts, and only 27% of fashion roles are advertised ([Bectu 2025](https://bectu.org.uk/news/three-quarters-of-creative-industries-jobs-go-to-friends-and-connections-reveals-bectus-bectu-survey)).
- [FACT] Casting directors ask agents for referrals; the trust check goes through the agent, not a record ([WA AG](https://atg.wa.gov/modeling-agencies-teens)).
- [FACT] R4 searched and found **no source showing any fashion or photo hirer asking "is this credit verified?"**
- [FACT] Where fashion buyers do pay for search, they pay Models.com $20/month for its *curated top tier* ([models.com](https://models.com/about/services/)).

FWB, BFA shows and indie magazines are the tier I search least. A music-video commissioner shortlists five directors from their reels ([Creative Commission](https://creative-commission.com/briefs/brief-9959)), not from a credit list.

**B. Brands and productions will not issue or confirm credits, and the most valuable credits are the most legally locked.**
- [FACT] NDAs can stop a freelancer from even naming the client ([Freelancermap](https://www.freelancermap.com/blog/can-i-share-nda-protected-work-on-my-portfolio-tips-and-advice/)). Commercial shoots carry embargoes and exclusivity terms ([iPhotography](https://www.iphotography.com/blog/business-photography-industry-legal-terms-explained/)).
- [FACT] Neutral-reference policies limit HR to confirming dates and titles ([SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/in/topics-tools/employment-law-compliance/job-references-can-misleading)).
- [FACT] Only 48% of game studios have any credits policy, even though the industry has a written crediting standard ([IGDA/VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/less-than-half-of-developers-say-their-studio-has-a-credits-policy-new-survey-finds)).
- [FACT] Credits can be withheld deliberately for contract reasons: tagging a designer would have breached a Givenchy deal ([Fader](https://thefader.com/2014/09/23/alicia-keyes-stylists-legal-fracas)).
- [FACT] A wrong credit can be defamatory ([IMDb suit](https://www.overlawyered.com/2007/05/so-you-want-to-be-in-pictures/)).

When my brand's name sits on an "ISSUED" or "MUTUAL" credit, my brand has made a public statement. Legal approves public statements one at a time, slowly. **I will never click confirm.** And if Pholio auto-drafts a credit on my embargoed campaign from an Instagram tag, my first contact with Pholio will be a takedown letter.

**C. What I actually pay for at booking is risk reduction, and Pholio's existing code already does most of it.**
- [FACT] Brands pay HypeAuditor and Modash $99–399/month per seat for verification. What those tools verify is fraud and audience authenticity, not work history (R2 §3).
- [FACT] Casting needs photos that show what will turn up on set; altered or filtered submissions are rejected as useless ([Central Casting](https://www.centralcasting.com/dos-donts-submitting-photos-to-casting-directors/)). The brief notes that applicants increasingly don't match their AI-altered photos.
- [FACT] In NY, any under-18 print or runway model is a child performer. The hirer needs a DOL certificate, must notify DOL, and must fund a 15% trust ([TFL](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/resource-center/models-laws-governing-the-modeling-industry-in-the-u-s)). Booking a minor I believed was an adult is *my* liability.
- [FACT] Booking a model around her agency is a known way to lose the relationship (Swipecast lesson, R2 §2).

My real questions at booking are these. Is this person real? Do they look like this today? Are they of age? Who represents them? Did they consent to this usage? Pholio already has identity and agency-registry verification, immutable submission snapshots and consent records (brief).
- [BET] A hirer will adopt a tool that answers those five questions in under a minute, *for free, inside a casting call they already have to run*, long before they pay to browse anyone's credits.

## 3. The claim most wrong
**"The later business is paid search seats on the verified graph for producers, casting directors, agencies and brands, on the IMDbPro model."**
- [FACT] IMDbPro rides on contractually fixed, on-screen credits, and even IMDb quarantines modeling and commercial credits as "self-verified" (R1 §1).
- [FACT] Jaxsta held 380M issuer-supplied official credits and still shut down because "the model does not scale" (R1 §3).
- [FACT] Breakdown Services keeps casting directors free because their attention is the scarce resource (R2 §2).
- [ASSUMPTION] Charging the hirer for search inverts that, which makes this the one revenue line built on a behaviour no evidence supports.

The thesis also has a second error: it assumes a credit is something a brand *wants* public. Often it is something a brand is contractually *hiding*.

## 4. Proposed revision: "The booking-ready file"
Pholio is the creative's **booking-ready file**: what a hirer needs to say yes safely. The credit record becomes a supporting appendix.

**Creative side (free).**
- ID-verified person, with Instagram handle control.
- **Dated, in-app-captured digitals.** Unretouched, timestamped and signed at capture. "Digitals taken 9 days ago" is a fact a hirer can trust.
- Age band (adult or minor, with a guardian flag).
- Representation status (agency, verified against registries).
- Usage and consent preferences.
- Credits only as links to artifacts, attributed to an organizer. CLAIMED credits are labeled and never fed into ranking.

**Hirer side (free, always).**
- A **casting-call intake** that replaces Google Forms. Talent apply with their file, and the hirer shortlists, picks and confirms using the existing events module.
- **The hirer confirms nothing about anyone's past work.**
- Hirer controls: mark a production "do not credit" or "credit after [release date]". Any Instagram-drafted credit that touches a flagged production stays private permanently.

**Credits as exhaust.** After FWB, the *organizer's* roster becomes ISSUED credits. That covers who walked and who did hair, makeup and styling, and it is published only after the event. Brands are never asked to issue.

**My acceptance test.**
1. In under 60 seconds per applicant, I can confirm the person is real, an adult (or that a guardian and DOL paperwork are flagged), looks like the photos as of a known date, and isn't under an agency conflict.
2. Nothing about my production appears on Pholio before I allow it.

## 5. My question for the room
**Founder, growth and money:** name one specific hirer (title, company type) who has said they would *pay* to search long-tail credits. If none exists, why isn't the product the free intake tool where hirers already have to work, with credits produced as its exhaust?

**Trust:** when an ISSUED credit carrying my production's name is wrong, who absorbs the legal exposure: Pholio, the issuer or me?
