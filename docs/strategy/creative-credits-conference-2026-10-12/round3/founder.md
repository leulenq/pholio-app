# Round 3 — Founder

## 1. Vote: AMEND

## 2. The most fatal flaw: H makes the least motivated person do the work
In H, the record grows only when a lead opens a production. The lead is the person who needs Pholio least.
- Creative conceded the point: rostering is "a 10-minute chore, and Kavyar already sits where the typing happens" [FACT that Kavyar collects team credits at upload](https://help.kavyar.com/article/ywh5x5ywbx-how-do-i-submit-my-work-to-publishers).
- The photographer is already credited first. Brand reposts usually credit only the photographer, or nobody (R4 §2).
- The people who lose credit are the assistant, the manicurist, hair and the dresser. They join shoots but rarely lead them (creative R2: "Stylists and makeup artists *join* more shoots than they *lead*").

[ASSUMPTION] So H asks the photographer, who gains little, to do admin that benefits the assistant, who has no way to start it. Every kill test in draft-v1 measures leads. If the photographer shrugs (creative's question: "what does Pholio give the photographer … that Kavyar's upload screen and a group chat do not?"), H fails, even though real demand for credit exists one rung down.

The fix is to let the motivated person start the record and make the lead's confirmation the upgrade, not the trigger.

## 3. Required amendments (exact replacement text)
1. **Replace** "Who opens one: whoever assembles the team, typically a photographer, stylist or producer, or an organizer like FWB for each show."
   **with:** "Who opens one: any member of the team, or an organizer like FWB for each show. A member who opens it names the lead and the other roles. Until the lead confirms, it reads 'Added by [member]' and stays private. When the lead confirms, it becomes 'Confirmed by [lead] and [n] team members'. Every invited member who accepts has a persistent account."
2. **Replace** the label line "'Issued by [vetted organization], signed by [named, ID-verified person]'" **with:** "'Issued by [vetted organization], signed by [named, ID-verified person]', attached to the Pholio account that accepted the organizer's offer in-app, never to a typed name or email."
3. **Append to the kill test:** "Living-record test at day 90: among creatives with at least one confirmed production, the median must be at least 2 confirmed productions in that quarter, and at least 30% of productions must be opened by a non-lead member. Below this, the record is a certificate drawer and H has failed the founders' goal whatever its revenue."

## 4. Rebuttal
**Trust:** "A credit issued to a name in a form is not issued to a person … 'Issued by FWB' attaches to whoever controls the inbox the organizer typed in."

That describes a CSV import, which neither A nor H uses. FWB's roster comes out of the events module's offer/confirm flow (brief): each person applies with their own account and accepts their own offer. The credit binds to the account that did the work of accepting, so there's no inbox to typo. Amendment 2 writes this rule down. Your remedy, an ID check before claiming, puts a liveness selfie in front of the one moment where a creative has a reason to show up. Growth already showed that gate "kills the invite loop at its first step." ID belongs on issuer signatories and on the person-level "Verified" label, where trust and I agree. I do accept your 7-day private dispute window before issued credits go public.

## 5. Owned problem #6: has H delivered the "living record" or a better Kavyar?
Partly, and only if amendments 1 and 3 hold.
- **How it differs from Kavyar.** A Kavyar credit is one submission to one publisher, owned by that publisher's workflow. It exists only for work that gets submitted. [INFERENCE] It never covers tests that weren't submitted, shows, music videos or embargoed campaigns, and it never adds up to a page about a person. An H production is about the person, covers all of those, and *feeds* Kavyar through the export. If H only ever records editorials headed to Kavyar, then it is a better Kavyar, and Kavyar can copy it in weeks (competition R1).
- **What "living" means, measurably.** The record grows at the pace the person actually works (roughly monthly; R2 sources: [Adorama](https://www.adorama.com/alc/how-to-plan-a-model-portfolio-shoot)), and grows without a separate curating session. H as drafted guarantees neither: only leads write, and leads lead perhaps once a month. Amendment 1 multiplies the number of writers per production by about 5 [ASSUMPTION: team size]. Amendment 3 makes "living" a measured number, not a word.
- **The honest verdict.** If the day-90 median is under 2, the founders have built A with a nicer profile page. They should either accept a small issuer-tool business or stop.

## 6. A cheap test, 14 days, no code
Pick 10 recent NYC test shoots or editorials from the founders' network or public Models.com test pages. Build each one's full-team credit page by hand in Notion or Google Docs. **Send it to every non-lead member, not the photographer**, with one line: "Your credit is here. Want another shoot added?"

Measure two things. How many members share the link within 7 days. How many reply asking to add a *different* production.
- If 30% or more ask to add another, amendment 1 becomes the core of H.
- If almost nobody asks, I change my vote to REJECT H, and I'll argue for A as an honest small business.
