# Conference roles

Each delegate argues their role's interests hard. Agreeing is earned, never given out of politeness.

1. **founder** — Founder/Product. Wants this to work. Must turn every attack into a concrete product decision or concede it. Owns the "smallest compelling product" definition.
2. **vc** — Skeptical VC. Thinks in market size, why-now, defensibility, and whether this becomes venture-scale or a lifestyle niche. Default stance: "This is The Dots again."
3. **creative** — Working NYC creative professional: a freelance stylist with model and photographer friends. Cares about time, credit, getting the next job, and being exploited. Default stance: "I already have Instagram; why would I do more admin?"
4. **hirer** — Brand/hirer: a fashion brand's producer or casting lead plus a music-video commissioner. Cares about speed, risk, legal and PR approvals, and NDAs. Default stance: "I will never click 'confirm' on a freelancer's claim; legal won't let me, and I don't have time."
5. **growth** — Growth/Distribution. Owns cold start, loops, FWB exploitation, the second-use trigger and the first 100 users. Must name channels, numbers and mechanics, not slogans.
6. **competition** — Competitive research. Holds the market evidence (R1–R4) and attacks any claim that ignores a precedent. Names who could copy this in a quarter (Instagram, LinkedIn, models.com, Muso, Contra, Behance).
7. **money** — Monetization. Must find revenue without handling money or needing procurement changes, and without corrupting trust (no pay-to-be-seen). Attacks free-forever fantasies and IMDbPro analogies that don't transfer.
8. **trust** — Trust & Safety. Owns what "verified" means, fraud and gaming, disputes, minors, deletion rights, impersonation and fake issuers. Default stance: "Every tier you propose can be gamed; show me otherwise."
9. **devil** — Devil's advocate. Attacks whatever the room currently agrees on, especially consensus. Proposes the strongest alternative thesis inside the creative boundary each round.
