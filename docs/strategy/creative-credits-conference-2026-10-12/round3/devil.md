# Round 3: Devil's advocate

## 1. Vote: **REJECT**
In round 2 I helped build H, and that was my failure. Here is the case against it.

## 2. Fatal flaw: H is The Dots, which the brief named as the precedent risk on page one
H's core object is "a production, the lead adds members by role, each member confirms." That is exactly the shape of products that already exist and that never became the standard:
- **The Dots:** "members post projects and tag the whole team" [FACT](https://techcrunch.com/2017/12/13/aiming-to-be-the-linkedin-for-creatives-the-dots-raises-4m/). It reached 300k to 1M members, never became the standard, and is now white-label software [FACT](https://londontechweek.com/speakers/pip-jamieson).
- **Visie:** "every project credits the full creative team," in fashion and photo, launched 2024 [FACT](https://visie.io/).
- **Behance co-owners:** accept-to-confirm, 56M members, not a hiring authority [FACT](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/204483494-Guide-Co-owning-And-Crediting).
- **Mosaic:** collaborator-verified credits [FACT](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/credits).

The only new parts of H are an embargo vault and an FWB stamp. Neither fixes what killed the others: **the record has no reader.**
- Founder's new evidence puts the lead's cadence at about **one test a month**, on DMs and Word call sheets [FACT](https://www.adorama.com/alc/how-to-plan-a-model-portfolio-shoot).
- The people who test most often are beginners doing TFP, which practitioners say "often produces weak work" [FACT](https://retouch4.me/blog/trade-for-print-or-time-for-print-photos-depending-on-who-you-ask-is-one-of-the-first-concepts-a-portrait-photographer-encounters-the-model-doesnt-get-paid-the-photographer-do).

So H's busiest writers record its least valuable credits, once a month, on pages nobody outside the team opens. The room killed search seats for lack of a reader, then built Dots-style credit pages that need one [INFERENCE].

## 3. Amendments (if the room overrides my REJECT)
1. Replace the thesis line with: **"Pholio is where creative opportunities are posted by verified posters, and the outcome of every opportunity is written into the creative's record by the poster."**
2. Replace "Launch: FWB runs each show as an issued production…" with: **"Launch: FWB, plus 10 verified NYC posters (agencies through the existing registry check, indie magazines, photographers casting tests), post real open calls on Pholio. Applicants apply with their Pholio. Selection and completion write credits issued by the poster."**
3. Replace the kill test with: **"Kill if, within 60 days, fewer than 3 non-FWB posters post a second opportunity unprompted, or the median opportunity draws fewer than 15 qualified applicants."**

## 4. Rebuttal
Founder: "Putting ID plus liveness in front of a free, exposure-tier open call adds friction exactly where FWB needs volume."
- FWB's scarce resource is a usable pool, not volume.
- Agencies already "receive hundreds of applications a week and don't respond" (brief).
- Bectu says fashion hiring is closed because open calls are noise: only 27% of fashion roles are advertised [FACT](https://bectu.org.uk/news/three-quarters-of-creative-industries-jobs-go-to-friends-and-connections-reveals-bectus-bectu-survey).
- Friction that filters the pool is the hirer's benefit. That said, my alternative puts mandatory ID on the **poster**, not the applicant. Poster verification is the anti-scam fix that FTC modeling-scam guidance points to [FACT](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/modeling-scams).

## 5. Problem #6 (mine): has H delivered a "living record"?
No. H delivers a better Kavyar caption box. A record stays living only if something *other than the creative's own admin* writes to it regularly.
- In H, the only writer is a lead with a monthly habit.
- In the **Opportunity Record**, the writer is every opportunity's outcome: applied, shortlisted, selected, worked, published. Each entry is issued by a verified poster, and the record grows whenever the creative pursues work, which they already do every week.
- The reader problem goes away because the poster *is* the reader: they read profiles to decide.
- Evidence of demand: Backstage reached about 250k paid subscriptions selling access to opportunities [FACT](https://flashesandflames.com/2021/02/25/backstage-expands-ex-us/). People pay for *access to gigs*. They do not pay for *records* (Jaxsta, R1 §3).
- Revenue with no money handling: verified posters' intake workspaces (agencies at $49–99/mo, per money's line), plus a creative Pro for presentation. Applying always stays free.
- Its weaknesses, stated honestly:
  - Opportunity boards attract scams; Backstage blocks about 280 per period [FACT](https://www.backstage.com/magazine/article/the-art-of-the-scam-how-grifters-operate-how-to-avoid-them-68015/).
  - It is a two-sided cold start.
  - Model Mayhem decayed in this same niche [ASSUMPTION].

  I would rather fight those weaknesses than The Dots' outcome.

## 6. Cheap 14-day test, no code
The founders run H by concierge:
- Recruit 15 NYC shoot leads.
- Give each a shared Google Sheet team template.
- Hand-build a credit page in Notion for each submitted shoot.

My vote flips to AMEND if both of these happen:
- ≥5 leads submit a **second** roster unprompted within 14 days.
- At least one credit page is opened by someone **outside the team** (link-click tracking).

If the record gets writers *and* a reader, H is not The Dots. If not, H is dead and the opportunity thesis is next.
