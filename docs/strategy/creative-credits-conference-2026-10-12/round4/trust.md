# Round 4 — Trust/safety: Mosaic is a direct competitor, not a trust straw man

**[BET] Vote: REJECT a generic verified-credit rebuild; permit a bounded portfolio-intake experiment with optional organizer-confirmed facts.** Better provenance is a necessary operating standard, not sufficient differentiation. If the only reason to choose Pholio is “our confirmations are more honest,” recommend Mosaic instead.

## Corrections and attacks

**Trust, including myself:** [ASSUMPTION, withdrawn] My Round 3 claimed that publication-design choices determine Section 230 protection, and Creative repeated that attributing statements to users keeps us protected. That is not established. [FACT] [Section 230](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/230) distinguishes another provider's information from responsibility for creating or developing information, and contains important exceptions. [BET] Attribution helps explain evidence; it is not a legal shield. Remove the categorical conclusion rather than build the business around it.

[BET, withdrawn] My compulsory ID-check rule for every creative lead was unsupported. An identity check can identify a liar without detecting their lie; it does not validate organizational authority or completed work. My categorical ban on exports used in visa applications also exceeded the evidence. [BET] A neutral export of user-controlled facts may be useful; do not advertise immigration sufficiency, generate recommendation letters, or certify eligibility. Whether a particular legal product is permissible needs specific analysis, not one fraud anecdote.

**Hirer:** [FACT] [Persona's processor policy](https://withpersona.com/legal/privacy-policy) identifies its customer as controller, describes facial-geometry processing on the customer's behalf, and ties retention to instructions. [ASSUMPTION, withdrawn] Vendor storage therefore does not eliminate our responsibilities. [BET] No face matching in the pilot. If identity checks later solve an observed impersonation problem, define the check, retention and jurisdictional obligations first. Neither biometric avoidance nor an ID pass certifies safety.

**Creative:** [ASSUMPTION] Your stranger-photographer use case confuses evidence of past collaborators with evidence of safe conduct. Someone may have real credits and still abuse people. A reference path is testable; a safety promise is indefensible on this record. Do not display “safe,” “trusted photographer,” or an implied clearance based on collaborator counts.

**Money:** [FACT: brief constraint] Production release capture is contracting and is excluded. [BET] Product publication permission is allowed as an operating requirement, but cannot replace image licenses, employment permissions or production releases. Stop rescuing weak credit demand by adding the excluded workflow.

## Mosaic, fairly read

[FACT] [Mosaic help](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/mosaic-help) documents a company-domain approver, project and role descriptions, approval before a public credit, and support for corrections. That is substantially stronger than reciprocal likes. [FACT] Its [October 2 terms](https://mosaic.creatorsguildofamerica.org/terms), sections 7–8, say approval reflects the responder's answer, details are not all independently checked, verification uses reasonable efforts, company email does not establish authority, and verification guarantees neither conduct nor accuracy. It already recognizes the limitations we claimed as our differentiator.

[ASSUMPTION] A malicious user could register a plausible fake-company domain, nominate a colluding employee, inflate an assistant role to director, or attach a real campaign to nonexistent participation. Compromised or shared genuine mailboxes are another route. These are threat scenarios, not evidence Mosaic has suffered them or fails to mitigate them. Rejecting Gmail does not solve them; insisting on government ID does not solve them either.

## Defensible record and workflow

[BET] Keep distinct fields: opportunity ID; organizer; specific show/project; actual work date; specific role; participant; statement author and capacity; approval timestamp; source category; publication permission; correction version. Preserve application, selected, accepted, checked-in, and organizer-confirmed completion as separate states. Check-in proves recorded presence; completion requires a bounded assertion of what was actually done. Neither implies payment, publication, quality, image rights or safety.

[BET] Onboard the pilot organizer through independently located official contact details. Record which authorized coordinator may attest which roles; domain ownership is one check, not authority. For completed FWB shows, reconcile available completed-role records and exceptions with that coordinator. Never turn every applicant or accepted person into a work credit.

[BET] Present “Organizer Jane Smith confirmed runway model participation in Designer X's show on [date], on [approval date].” Show evidence scope and unresolved gaps. Ask whether participation required a fee; distinguish participant-paid, volunteer, paid work and unknown as factual organizer/participant assertions without amounts. Paying a designer to exhibit does not automatically describe every model's arrangement. Contradictory answers pause publication and prompt clarification; Pholio handles no funds or payment dispute.

[BET] Obtain explicit individual permission before public naming. Silence is never consent. Keep pilot public records adult-only, treating an age declaration as a declaration, not proven age. Invitations are transactional, rate-limited, removable and never an automatic marketing enrollment. Private schedules, contact details and embargoed client information remain restricted.

[BET] Any named person can request removal or dispute a specific field. Suppress contested public attribution while reviewing, notify the submitter and confirmer, inspect permitted evidence, and record a dated correction or withdrawal. Allow appeal; avoid public accusations inferred from complaints. Maintain minimal access-controlled history with a stated retention period.

## Why Pholio instead?

[BET] Only an observed selection-coordination advantage gives a reason: reusable portfolio submissions, authorized designer review, acceptance reconciliation, then optional completed-work attribution. Mosaic can remain the creator's record. No documented intake feature does not prove Mosaic lacks one or cannot add it. Require a real organizer to choose and pay for this workflow over their current tools. If that fails, a cleaner verification vocabulary is no business.
