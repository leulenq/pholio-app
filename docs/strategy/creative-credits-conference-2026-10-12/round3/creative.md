# Round 3: Creative

## 1. Vote: AMEND

## 2. H's fatal flaw: every output arrives after the work, so Pholio becomes the second place a team gets typed in
Every H output (credit page, paste block, Kavyar export, embargo vault) comes after the shoot. By then the roster already exists in DMs, and for editorials it is typed into Kavyar at upload [FACT](https://help.kavyar.com/article/ywh5x5ywbx-how-do-i-submit-my-work-to-publishers). Re-entering data after the fact is the chore pattern that killed Polywork and The Dots [FACT, R1 Patterns 1, 10]. On top of that, H charges the lead for "unlimited productions … evidence packs." That makes my credit as a member depend on whether my photographer pays, and it puts a price on credibility, which is Flanelle's model and not ours.

**Competition's question: why type credits into Pholio before Kavyar?** For a photographer whose only output is Kavyar editorials, there is no reason, and I concede that. There are four places where Pholio comes first:
1. **Before the shoot.** The team list starts as a call sheet: date, call time, address, roles, looks. Today it lives in Notes, Google Docs or StudioBinder, and none of those has a handles field [FACT](https://www.studiobinder.com/photography-call-sheet). If Pholio is the call-sheet link dropped in the group chat, the credits are just that same sheet filled in.
2. **The stylist holds what the photographer doesn't.** Kavyar *requires* wardrobe credits [FACT, Kavyar link above]. The stylist knows every pull and label and lives in the group chat begging the photographer to credit them correctly. [ASSUMPTION] The stylist is Pholio's natural lead, and Kavyar gives stylists no way to start a submission because only the copyright holder uploads.
3. **Most work never reaches Kavyar.** Lookbooks, music videos, client work, tests and runway don't go there. [ASSUMPTION] Kavyar also rejects many submissions, and a rejected editorial is still real work.
4. **Kavyar credits are typed by the uploader.** I never confirm my own role or the spelling of my handle there.

## 3. Amendments
1. **Outputs, add as the first bullet:** "A pre-shoot call sheet link (date, call time, roles, looks; address visible only to confirmed members). A production opens when the team is booked; the credit page is the same object after release."
2. **Money, replace the lead-pays line with:** "Opening productions, confirming, credit pages, embargo vault and evidence packs are free for everyone. Lead creatives pay about $12/mo only for workflow and presentation extras (call-sheet templates, client-branded share pages, custom domain, archive export). No member's credit ever depends on whether the lead pays."
3. **Kill test, add:** "In the 10 observed shoots, record where the team list is first written and when. If in 7 or more of 10 it is first written at Kavyar upload, cut the pre-shoot moment, and H reduces to A."

## 4. Rebuttals
- **VC:** "'invited people confirm with one tap and no account.' That is good UX and a dead network." Agreed on the stub, and I amend: the tap creates a claimable stub keyed to the member's handle or phone, and the next invite lands there. I won't move on forced signup. Requiring an account to be credited for work you already did is the friction that made Kavyar team credits uploader-typed in the first place, and it would collapse acceptance.
- **Trust:** "Vouching *is* liability." Fair, and I withdraw "Pholio vouches." Pholio vouches for nothing. It keeps a ledger of who said what, when: "[lead] listed you as stylist; you confirmed." The statement belongs to them, not us, which also keeps us on the Section 230 side [FACT, R3 §6].

## 5. Problems I own
- **#1 Frequency.** [GAP] There is still no data. [ASSUMPTION] Leads run about 1–4 shoots a month, but members join more. So measure *productions joined per creative*, not only productions led. A stylist or makeup artist on 4–8 sets a month is the real habit, and the lead is just the person who opens the sheet.
- **#4 What "credible" buys, concretely:**
  1. **Safety.** A model decides whether to show up to a stranger's test shoot. [ASSUMPTION] Models are told to check a photographer's past collaborators, and today that means DMing girls from his grid. A confirmed team history is a real check with real stakes, and it serves the scam problem in R3.
  2. **Agency applications**, where the existing intake code already applies.
  3. **Showroom and PR pulls**, using the credit page link, since pull letters are cut.
  4. **A new collaborator** deciding whether to accept a TFP.
  5. **O-1B evidence** [FACT, R4 §1].

  Paying hirers are not on this list, and that is fine.

## 6. A 14-day, no-code test
Get 10 NYC photographers and stylists to show their last 90 days of shoots: the count, where the team list lived, whether each went to Kavyar, and who wrote the credits. Ask 20 models: "Before a test with someone new, how do you check them?"
- If productions led are under 1 a month and most rosters are first written at Kavyar, I vote REJECT and back A.
- If models already vet photographers by DMing past collaborators, I vote ACCEPT.
