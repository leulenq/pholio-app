# Round 3: Trust & Safety (trust)

## 1. Vote: AMEND
H is the first version of this thesis that I can defend. One clause in it publishes named people who never agreed to be published.

## 2. Fatal flaw: "or a timeout has passed"
H makes a production public once "every listed member has confirmed **or a timeout has passed**." Under that rule, silence counts as consent. The lead types in names, and after the timeout those names go public under the lead's assertion. Three consequences follow:
- **Minors.** H cuts "public credits for minors," but members are not ID-checked, so H cannot tell who is a minor. In NY, an under-18 runway or print model is a child performer ([FACT](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/resource-center/models-laws-governing-the-modeling-industry-in-the-u-s)). A timeout would publish a 16-year-old's name, role and shoot date with nothing to stop it.
- **Deliberate omissions and disputes.** Some credits are withheld on purpose for contract reasons ([FACT](https://thefader.com/2014/09/23/alicia-keyes-stylists-legal-fracas)), and stylist credit fights are routine ([FACT](https://www.bellanaija.com/?p=421342)). A timeout publishes the lead's version over the silent member's.
- **Liability.** A lead-authored statement about a non-consenting person is the kind of content that has been litigated as defamation ([FACT](https://www.overlawyered.com/2007/05/so-you-want-to-be-in-pictures/)). [ASSUMPTION] Pholio stays a host under Section 230 only if it does not design the publication rule that turns silence into a public claim.

## 3. Required amendments (exact replacement text)
**A1.** Replace "Nothing is public until (a) every listed member has confirmed or a timeout has passed AND (b) the work is publicly released" with:
> "A member's name appears publicly only after that member confirms. Members who have not confirmed appear as their role alone ('Hair: not confirmed'). Silence is never consent. Any member can remove their own name at any time, and the lead cannot restore it. Nothing is public before the work is publicly released."

**A2.** Replace "public credits for minors" (in Cut from v1) with:
> "Every member attests 18+ when confirming. With no attestation, or if under 18, the credit stays private to that member. Organizer intakes collect date of birth, and under-18s never appear on public pages."

**A3.** Replace "evidence packs" (in Pro) with:
> "a free, dated export of the person's own record, with no Pholio attestation language."
Pholio must not sell a document that people put into O-1B filings. A falsified letter has already got a model's O-1B revoked ([FACT](https://meritdraft.com/decisions/o-1b-dismissed-fashion-model-2018-03-30.html)).

## 4. Rebuttal
Growth: *"Putting a roughly $1 Persona check and a liveness selfie in front of the accept tap kills the invite loop at its first step."*
**Conceded in part; the principle holds.** Accepting stays free and ID-free. The ID check moves to the lead, who opens the production and pays $12. What growth's version leaves open is a lead running a ring of unverified sock puppets that "confirm" a fake shoot (FTC: modeling scams run on fake shoots, [FACT](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/modeling-scams)). The answer is to make the confirmers visible, not to ID-check every member. See #3.

## 5. Resolving #3: is "Confirmed by lead + n" MUTUAL?
**Yes, it is mutual, and I withdraw my "cut MUTUAL."** What I was cutting in Round 1 was MUTUAL as a *trust tier*: a level of verification, rankable and sellable, where n confirmations make a credit "more verified." That stays cut. What H needs is MUTUAL as a *provenance sentence*: who said so, shown in full, with nothing added up. The rules:
1. **There is no count and no tier.** The page lists each confirmer: "Confirmed by @lead (ID-checked), @a, @b (not ID-checked, joined 3 days ago)." It never says "confirmed by 5." Account age and ID status are shown because they are cheap, proven fraud tells ([FACT](https://www.kaspersky.com/blog/beware-of-twitter-blue-fake-accounts/49199/)).
2. **The lead must be ID-checked** for any creative-led production to go public.
3. **Confirmations never feed ranking, search or any paid layer.** Raw peer signals are unusable without about 84 models of credibility ([FACT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/social-business/linkedins-looking-make-endorsements-more-relevant-heres-how)), and we will not build those models.
4. **The lead carries a reputation.** When a member disputes a role, the dispute shows in plain text ("Role disputed by @x"). A lead with substantiated fake productions loses publishing rights on every production they lead.

So there are two trust-weighted facts, *Issued by [org], signed by [person]* and *[Person] is ID-checked*. Everything else is attribution the reader judges.

## 6. 14-day no-code test
Take FWB's last-season roster plus the last 3 shoots of 5 friendly photographers or stylists, built by hand as Google Doc team sheets. DM each named member a confirmation request. Measure:
1. the share who confirm within 7 days;
2. the share who object to being named, or who dispute a role;
3. how many are under 18 or will not state their age.

**Vote rule.** If objections plus disputes exceed 10%, or confirmations fall below 50%, peer confirmation is too lossy to publish and I move to REJECT H in favor of issuer-only public pages. [BET on thresholds]
