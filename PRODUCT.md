# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Talent users (models, creatives) building professional portfolios and applying to agencies. Agency users managing rosters, reviewing applications, and running casting operations. Both are industry professionals who expect polish and credibility from every touchpoint.

## Product Purpose

Pholio is a full-stack talent portfolio and agency management platform. Talent creates portfolios, generates PDF comp cards, and applies to agencies. Agencies manage rosters, run casting, and track commissions. Success looks like a platform that feels as premium as the talent industry it serves.

## Brand Personality

Editorial. Earned. Exact.

The brand sits at the intersection of fashion editorial aesthetics and professional SaaS utility. Warm gold against near-black. Noto Serif Display for display moments; Inter for functional UI. The mark — a serif "P." — signals craft without announcing it.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS (Notion, Linear): too flat, too neutral, no industry identity
- Flashy entertainment agency sites (bold neons, full-bleed model photos with no restraint): loud, not premium
- Corporate enterprise software: institutional gray, no soul
- Fashion-house ultra-minimalism (LVMH style): too sparse, too much white space, not enough utility

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth through accent, not background** — gold and serif typography carry warmth; backgrounds stay near-black or warm-neutral, not beige by default
2. **Every surface earns its keep** — no decorative patterns, badges, or glass cards; every element has a functional role
3. **Precision at every scale** — from 16px favicon to full-screen layout, proportions and legibility are non-negotiable
4. **Editorial confidence** — headings in Noto Serif Display or Playfair, generous tracking on wordmarks, unhurried hierarchy
5. **Motion that reveals, not distracts** — spring-based entrances (Framer Motion), transitions that serve navigation, no animation loops

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Gold (#C9A55A) on near-black (#050505) exceeds 4.5:1 for large text. Body copy uses near-black on cream surfaces. Reduced-motion media query respected for all transitions and animations.
