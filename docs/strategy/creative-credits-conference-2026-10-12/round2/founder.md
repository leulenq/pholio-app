# Round 2 — Founder: attack

## 1. Attacking Candidate A (my own Round 1 position)
The moderator is right that A quietly drops the goal. Three specific failures:
- **Not living.** A record that only an organizer can write updates when organizers run events. FWB runs two seasons a year plus a 6–7-designer Queens week [FACT](https://qns.com/2026/07/queens-center-launch-fashion-week-queens/). Growth's point holds: a model credited in October gets nothing until April. My own Round 1 kill metric (≥40% claim their profile) measures one visit, not a record.
- **The revenue is too small.** Money's own pricing, "$20 per designer slot … FWB's ~40 slots come to about $800 a season", makes the flagship customer worth about $1,600 a year. [ASSUMPTION] Reaching $1M ARR at that price needs roughly 600 FWB-sized organizers, and nobody in this room has named five in NYC.
- **Organizers are nonprofits running on exposure.** BK Style is a 501(c)(3), and designers at Brooklyn Made get "exposure", not a stipend [FACT](https://brooklynmadestore.com/pages/brooklyn-fashion-show-2026). A is event SaaS sold to the poorest buyer in the chain, and Kavyar can add "published in" credits in weeks (competition, Round 1).

What A gets right, and what survives: the record must be written as a by-product of assembling a team, and a named, accountable party must sign it.

## 2. Attacking named delegates
- **VC: "≥5 organizers outside the founders' network pay ≥$1,500/season."** That test hands A a verdict it can only fail. Money prices FWB, the best case, at $800. Passing the test would still describe a tool business, which VC himself concedes. A real venture test has to measure whether the *record* compounds between events, which VC's three criteria never touch.
- **Devil/hirer (C): "Digitals and self-tapes are shot inside Pholio … ID-matched."** Digitals serve one role (model or actor) out of the roughly ten on an FWB roster: stylists, HMU, dressers, photographers. Putting ID plus liveness in front of a free, exposure-tier open call adds friction exactly where FWB needs volume. R3 found no drop-off data, so C is betting blind on its core funnel step. The identity layer is also the most copyable piece: Meta Verified and CLEAR already sell it (R3 §2, §4). I'll keep dated, in-app digitals as a model-profile feature, not as the thesis.
- **Growth: "Each runs about 2 shoots a month with about 5 other people … 60% acceptance."** New evidence partly supports this. Working fashion photographers describe testing about **once a month**, and Lindsay Adler recommends monthly test days [FACT](https://www.adorama.com/alc/how-to-plan-a-model-portfolio-shoot), [FACT](https://www.diyphotography.net/lindsay-adler-gives-her-top-5-tips-to-immediately-improve-your-fashion-photography/). Halve growth's cadence. The tooling today is DMs, Model Mayhem castings, Word call sheets and Milanote boards [FACT](https://petapixel.com/2015/01/13/8-steps-arranging-fashion-editorial-shoot), [FACT](https://milanote.com/guide/photoshoot-call-sheet). StudioBinder and SetHero cost $19–29/month and are built for film crews [FACT, vendor/rival claims](https://sethero.com/?p=11823). [GAP] I found no survey of NYC shoot frequency. So rosters do recur, monthly, on improvised tools, and there's no purpose-built tool at the test-shoot tier.
- **Competition: "Kill … MUTUAL."** I said the same in Round 1, and it was wrong. Without a creative-written confirmed tier, the only writers are institutions, and the record can't be living.

## 4. Revised position (what changed)
**Pholio is where a creative team is assembled and confirmed. Every production (a shoot, show, editorial or video) leaves a signed credit page, and every person's portfolio is the sum of the productions they were confirmed on.**

- **Changed: the core object** is the *production*, not the event. FWB is the first and highest-authority producer, not the business. The same primitive (roster, roles, invite, accept, consent, publish) serves a photographer's monthly test and FWB's 40 shows. Most of it already exists in the events module.
- **Changed: MUTUAL reinstated**, renamed per trust's table. It reads "Confirmed by [lead, role] and [n] team members". It is never called verified, and it is never in any paid layer. "Issued by [org]" stays the top label, with trust's issuer gates.
- **Added:** creative's embargo vault, where confirmed credits go public on a release date, and the paste-ready credit block. Brands are never asked to confirm anything (hirer).
- **Revenue, in sequence:** organizer desk paid per season for institutions (money's Show Desk, priced honestly as small); a lead Pro tier ([BET] about $10/month) for photographers and stylists running ≥2 productions a month, covering call sheets, pull-letter drafts and Kavyar-ready exports. Search seats stay cut.
- **Held:** no Instagram drafting, no ratings, no under-18 public credits, publication only after the event.
- **Kill criteria:** growth's test, at ≥5 of 15 seed leads creating a second production unprompted within 45 days (not 30, given the monthly cadence), plus creative's test (≥20% of FWB photographers and stylists open a non-FWB production within 45 days). If both fail, Pholio is A, and the founders should decide whether a small tool business is acceptable.
