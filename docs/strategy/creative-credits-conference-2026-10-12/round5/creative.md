## The Mosaic counterfactual

[FACT] [Mosaic help](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/mosaic-help) documents free contributor-initiated credit requests, role descriptions, company-domain approvers and collaborators. These are distinct from paid guild membership.

[BET] If Mosaic offered free bulk FWB issuance tomorrow, **Pholio would not deserve to exist for the credits thesis tested here**. An assistant should use the existing tool if it meets their need. A hypothetical designer-selection reconciliation job has a coordinator reader and organizer payer, but it is a separate unproven product—not the remaining part of creative identity.

## Reader, return and falsification

[ASSUMPTION] The credible scarce reader for an assistant is a producer or photographer actually staffing a shoot and checking relevant experience or references. The assistant returns when applying for another real job or needing another contribution recorded. No cadence is known. An excluded assistant can initiate Mosaic's request; an inaccessible organizational approver may still block it. Personal-email shoots remain a possible boundary, not established valuable demand.

[BET] Recruit no users during this conference. Later, only with authorization, observe five live assistant-to-producer submissions with a concrete unresolved role or reference question. Compare existing CV/portfolio/reference evidence with Mosaic-assisted attribution, including approval effort and failed requests. Precommit: two documented useful checks or net time savings warrant further investigation; none means stop the attribution thesis. These are exploration decisions, not benchmarks. Publishing, clicks and courtesy confirmation do not count.

[BET] Strongest alternative: STOP THESIS now. Flip toward BUILD NARROW only if repeated consequential cases fail in Mosaic and ordinary evidence, and a real reader chooses a remedy. No safety certification, unsupported integration or generic SaaS retreat earns that flip.
