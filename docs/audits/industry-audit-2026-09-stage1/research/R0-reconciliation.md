Corrections to my own priors:
- I overestimated how universal "digitals" and "kept on file" are on the public intake surface.
- I underestimated the board-as-desk semantics; "signing board" is a genuine collision, not a nit.
- I assumed agencies want a status pipeline; the evidence says they want near-nothing pre-signing and
  delete data on a clock. A rich agency-side pipeline is a product bet, not an industry mirror.
- Payment term "45 days" and "Art. 11" were wrong in my brief; FWA is Art. 36, payment term is deal-memo defined.
