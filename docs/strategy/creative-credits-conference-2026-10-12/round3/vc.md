# Round 3: VC

## 1. Vote: AMEND
H is the best product shape this room has produced. As written, it is also a $300k business with no reader.

## 2. The most fatal flaw: H has supply and no demand, so its revenue is capped at Linktree prices
Every H output is written *by* creatives *for* creatives: credit pages, paste blocks, a Kavyar export, a vault. Devil asked in Round 2 who reads the record, and nobody answered: "They killed search seats for lack of a reader and then kept the page that needs one." Without a reader, the only payer is the lead, buying tools.

That lead tests about **once a month** [FACT](https://www.adorama.com/alc/how-to-plan-a-model-portfolio-shoot), per founder's own Round 2 evidence. One production a month does not justify $12/mo when Word and Milanote are free [ASSUMPTION].

**Verdict:** Without an opportunity layer, no version of H is venture-scale. H plus a creative Pro tier tops out near Muso, about $2M. The one proven nine-figure-adjacent model in this space has creatives paying for **access to open calls and submissions**: Backstage, Actors Access, Mandy, Spotlight, and Kavyar's own PRO and ELITE tiers. It never has buyers paying to search.

Here is the expansion path [BET]:
1. FWB's open call runs on Pholio.
2. Vetted magazines, test-shoot leads and agencies post calls there for free.
3. Creatives apply *with* their production record.
4. The production record then has a reader: the person reviewing the applications.
5. The path widens from fashion to every talent-neutral production type: music video, content, theater. That matches the founders' "all talent" scope.

Even if every step works, the outcome is a $20–45M ARR category leader, not a unicorn. The founders should hear that number plainly.

## 4. Required amendments (replacement text)

**Replace the "Money:" bullets with:**
> Money: Members and the record stay free forever. Lead Pro ($12/mo: unlimited productions, vault, exports) is the first revenue line, not the business. The scale line is **Open Calls**: vetted organizers, magazines, agencies and shoot leads post calls for free, and creatives apply with their Pholio record. The free tier includes a fixed number of applications each month. Pro includes unlimited applications and submission packs. Payment never changes ranking, labels, order or visibility on the reviewer's side, and reviewers never pay to see applicants.

**Add to "Outputs:"**
> - an application view, where whoever reviews an open call sees each applicant's confirmed productions, dated digitals and labels in one screen. This is the record's reader.

**Append to "Kill test":**
> Venture gate: do not raise until at least 1,000 monthly active creatives outside the FWB cohort, at least 25% of FWB-credited creatives applying to a non-FWB call within 60 days, and at least 4% paid conversion. If the gate is missed, run Pholio as a lean tool business.

## 5. Rebuttals
**Money:** "Passing that test proves $15k ARR. The VC's own kill criterion can't tell a venture apart from a hobby."

That is correct, and it was deliberate. A kill test is built to *fail cheaply*, not to prove upside. Its job was to stop the founders from raising on A, and money's own table now agrees that A is a hobby. The real flaw was that I had no upside test at all. The venture gate above fixes that.

**Devil:** "Nothing in that path starts from a Brooklyn nonprofit's Google Form."

I concede the Talent Systems analogy for the organizer side. But the money in that category comes from the supply side paying for access to opportunities (Backstage at 250k paid subscribers, Spotlight). A niche open-call feed is exactly where that money starts. FWB's Google Form is an open call, so it is a legitimate first call on that feed.

## 6. One cheap test, 14 days, no code
Founders post 3 real open calls in their network through a Typeform: one FWB-adjacent casting and two test shoots from seeded leads. Afterwards, every applicant gets a Stripe Payment Link offer: "$12 for unlimited applications to the next 10 Pholio calls."

- **Flips my vote to ACCEPT:** at least 5% of applicants prepay, *and* the leads report that the applicant records changed who they picked.
- **Moves me to REJECT the venture framing:** fewer than 1% prepay.
