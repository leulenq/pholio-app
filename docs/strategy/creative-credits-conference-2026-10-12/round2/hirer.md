# Round 2: HIRER (brand producer / casting lead / music-video commissioner)

## 1. Attack on Candidate A (Organizer/Issuer Desk)

**A serves the organizer and leaves the hirer out.** At FWB the hirer is a designer choosing from FWB's own open call. The hirer who pays day rates (a brand, an agency client, a label commissioner) gets an "ISSUED by a regional fashion week" line.
- [ASSUMPTION] To me that reads as *emerging, unpaid, exposure-tier*. Devil made the same point.
- [FACT] R4 Against #5: hirers who pay for search are mostly hiring from the tier Models.com already covers.
- A records the *event*. It doesn't answer my booking questions: is this person real, do they look like this today, are they of age, who represents them. FWB's signatory can attest that someone walked. FWB cannot attest an ID, an age or a current face, and trust's question ("who at BK Style signs, and what do they lose?") is still unanswered.

**A's expansion path goes through my legal department.** VC: "later music-video and brand productions." That means I run my casting and roster on Pholio and then publish crew credits. My productions carry NDAs, embargoes and exclusivity ([FACT](https://www.freelancermap.com/blog/can-i-share-nda-protected-work-on-my-portfolio-tips-and-advice/); [FACT](https://www.iphotography.com/blog/business-photography-industry-legal-terms-explained/)). Brands will not become issuers. A's growth ceiling is therefore the nonprofit and indie tier.

**The roster job A claims is already tooled where real productions do it.**
- [FACT] Productions build crew lists and call sheets in StudioBinder, with one-click RSVP and tracking of confirmations ([StudioBinder](https://www.studiobinder.com/call-sheet-builder/); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2015-02-02/studio-binder-lets-photographers-send-track-call-times-ease/)). SetHero, Yamdu and CallSheet do the same ([AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/callsheet-crew-calls/)).
- [FACT] Fashion casting management also exists already: I-Cast (Milan) and CastConnectPro ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/i-cast)).
- Answer to the moderator's research question: yes, rosters are assembled again and again, but by *producers*, in call-sheet tools, and those rosters are confidential working documents, not credit pages. Google Forms persists only at the unfunded edge (FWB). A wins the tier that has the least money.

## 2. Attacks on named delegates

**Creative:** "confirmed team credits on NDA work stay private and go public on a release date *I* set."
- The release date of my campaign is *mine*, under contract. A freelancer-set date that publishes a team list naming my brand before launch is a breach that Pholio would be hosting.
- [FACT] Credits are withheld deliberately for contract reasons ([Fader](https://thefader.com/2014/09/23/alicia-keyes-stylists-legal-fracas)).
- Fix: the vault's default is "never public." It opens only on a *public artifact* (the published campaign URL) or the client's own release, never on a date the creative picks.

**Money:** "The seat buys *contact*: messaging creatives requires an ID-verified hirer account plus a card on file."
- I don't cold-message talent. I go through agents ([FACT](https://atg.wa.gov/modeling-agencies-teens)).
- [FACT] R3 §7: a messaging or "opportunity" layer is the highest-risk scam surface, and scammers target people *because* they have credits.
- A $20 card on file is a toll, not a filter. Scammers already pay $625-scale fees to run fake shows. Paid contact is the Snapcast pattern from the other side.

**Devil** (my closest ally): the kill test "≥30% of casting decisions change because of a mismatch Pholio flagged."
- That measures the wrong thing. Most applicants at a curated open call are real, so a 30% mismatch rate would mean FWB's pool is fraud-ridden, and the test would fail even if C works.
- [BET] The right metric is my time: median minutes from application to shortlist decision, and the share of shortlisted people who arrive looking like their file.

## 4. Revised position

**I concede** that C on its own has no distribution. No hirer will ask for a "Pholio file" until something they already use delivers it. A's open call is that vehicle.

**I hold that**
1. The hirer never confirms, issues or pays to search.
2. Brands are never issuers.
3. Embargo control belongs to the client or the public artifact, never the creative.

**Revision ("the Verified Open Call").**
- Pholio's open call (A) *requires* the booking-ready file (C): ID match, capture-dated digitals or self-tape, age band, representation, consent.
- Hirers (designers, agencies, brand casting) shortlist from frozen snapshots for free.
- After the work becomes public, the *organizer's* roster issues credits.
- The same file is reused at every next application, so creatives come back because opportunities ask for it, not because they're maintaining a record.
- Team sheets (B) stay private working documents. They become public only when a public artifact exists.
- Revenue: organizer and agency intake workspaces (money's Payer 1, extended to agencies drowning in applications). No hirer seats.

**Kill test [BET].** In FWB's next open call:
- Designers' shortlist time falls by ≥50% against Google Forms.
- ≥1 NYC agency adopts the file as its *required* online application within 90 days.

If both fail, hirers don't value the file, and I'll say so.
