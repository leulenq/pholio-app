# Pholio for agencies — narrative

Audience: agency owner, director, booker, casting lead. Seller's claim: Pholio is a better front door for talent.
Test per slide: what does the agency believe after it that it did not before?

## Act I — Your front door today
1. **Cover.** "A better front door for talent." — This is about my intake, not a talent app.
2. **The click.** Someone taps Apply on your site. Everything after that is a system nobody designed. — Intake is a workflow, not a form.
3. **The workflow map.** Publish → Arrive → Collect → Review → Follow up → Close. Under each: where it lives today (site form / Google Form / email / IG DM / spreadsheet / nobody). — My intake runs across 5+ tools and the gaps are where time goes.
4. **What arrives.** An inbox of realistic submissions: HEIC attachment, filtered selfie, "5'9 i think", IG link instead of photos, 14 MB file, missing email. — Applicants don't send what I ask for.
5. **Your form doesn't enforce it either.** Real captured forms (Aug 19, 2026): Q says JPG ≤3MB, accepted a 4 MB file and a .txt; Elite hides HEIC but doesn't block it; ONE caps 600 KB; Muse ~1 MB by email; most forms: no size cap. — This is industry-wide; the form is not a filter.
6. **Review at volume.** ~100 a day at one agency, about 1% called back (reported). Published FAQ lines: Muse, ONE, Wilhelmina. — Silence became policy because the tools can't carry the load.
7. **Follow-up breaks.** A promising applicant → "Can you send unretouched digitals?" → reply 9 days later, a different address, a different thread → who asked? — The expensive part is following through, not first screening.
8. **Your front door is being copied.** ONE's banner "PROTECT YOURSELF FROM IMPERSONATORS…", Wilhelmina's "WARNING REGARDING IMPOSTERS", womenmanagment.com. — An unofficial, scattered front door is a liability to my name.

## Act II — Pholio, used by Meridian for one week (fictional agency)
9. **Pholio is the front door.** One official link → prepared applicants → one room to decide → exports to your tools. Free. — There's a single thing to adopt.
10. **Two sides.** Talent keep a professional record; agencies get complete submissions; agencies answer; talent keep records current. — Talent have a reason to show up prepared.
11. **Monday 9:00 — publish.** Open call brief: who it's for, what to send, eligibility, what happens next. [screen] — Setting up a call is a brief, not a build.
12. **The link goes where you already post.** app.pholio.studio/opencall/… on site, IG bio, casting post. Pause · resume · revoke. — Adoption = paste a link.
13. **Requirements, stated once.** Requirements builder: shots, how pictures should look, file limits. [screen] — Applicants see my rules before they send.
14. **The applicant arrives.** Arrival page, mobile. [screens] — The experience reflects well on my agency.
15. **They arrive with a record.** Talent side: profile, digitals, measurements, readiness check before sending. [screen] — Submissions show up complete.
16. **Tuesday — the desk.** Submissions: every card the same shape — face, height, age, city; "No digitals" flagged. [screen] — I can scan a week in minutes.
17. **One applicant, one decision.** Review Room stage. [screen] — Everything to decide is on one screen, snapshot as sent.
18. **The vocabulary you already use.** Keep on file · Request digitals · Invite to meet · Shortlist · Offer · Pass. Pass with a templated reason + internal note. [screen] — Decisions take a keystroke and reach talent properly.
19. **Nobody waits forever.** 30-day window (1–120 or off), daily; the exact text talent receive. — Silence is handled for me, honestly.
20. **Follow-up in one place.** Digitals refresh request; talent reply by email without logging in. [screen] — Follow-up stops leaking across inboxes.
21. **Thursday — the board.** Signing Board wall + line-up (up to 6, no score or rank). [screens] — From intake to signing without a spreadsheet.

## Act III — Casting organizations
22. **Cast a show from one pool.** Event call with required compensation, 18+ statement → organizer pool → designer pick lists (no login, measurements only) → offers with 72h response → CSV per designer. [screens] — Event casting fits the same front door.

## Act IV — Running it
23. **Your team.** Owner, Admin, Agent, Scout, Viewer. — Access is controlled.
24. **It leaves when you want.** CSV columns; signed webhook, 10 retries. Keeps your agency software. — Not a second system of record.
25. **Before / after.** Same week, two workflows. — The change is concrete.

## Act V — Objections
26. **"Why change our form?"** You don't have to delete it. Run a Pholio link beside it for one call; compare. — Low-risk trial.
27. **"Does talent need Pholio already?"** No. Anyone applies from the link — no account needed, about four minutes on a phone. Afterwards they can claim what they sent as a Pholio profile. Those already on Pholio apply with the record they keep. — My applicant pool isn't limited.
28. **"Does it add friction?"** One question per screen, photos last, no account. Fewer dead ends than a form that accepts anything and loses half of it. Show the mobile sequence. — Applying stays easy; what arrives is better.
29. **"What does it replace / coexist with?"** Replaces: form, intake inbox, reply tracking, decline emails. Coexists: agency software, website, Instagram, open calls in person. — Nothing to migrate.
30. **"What does it cost?"** Nothing. No agency billing exists. Talent never pay to apply or be seen. — No budget conversation.
31. **"Who sees the data? Any AI judging applicants?"** Role permissions; no score, rank or best match; designers see measurements only; adults only at launch; discoverability opt-in. — Safe to put my name on.
