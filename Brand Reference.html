Chapter I — The Lockup

The wordmark is
two gestures, not one.

The Pholio logo is not a serif word in gold. It is a serif word in gold plus the gold sweep beneath it — the same gesture that completes the preloader. The sweep is part of the brand expression, not an animation flourish. The lockup is incomplete without it.

PHOLIO
Primary · On Ink Noto Serif Display 400 · 64px · 0.20em · #C8A96E · sweep 180px

Size variants.

Four sizes only. The proportion of word to sweep is fixed: the sweep is always roughly half the wordmark's optical width, centered.

Construction specs.

Wordmark
Noto Serif Display, weight 400, all-caps, set in PHOLIO. Never weight 500+. Never italic.
Letter-spacing
0.20em at all sizes ≥ 24px. 0.22em at small (≤ 16px) to preserve openness.
Color on ink
#C8A96E (Gold Warm). Slightly warmer than paper-gold; reads cooler under ambient glow.
Color on cream
#C9A55A (Gold). The system's canonical gold. Use everywhere except the preloader and ink heroes.
The sweep
1px height. Width is ~50% of the wordmark's optical width. Horizontal gradient transparent → gold → transparent. Centered. Sits 4–16px below the baseline depending on size.
Clear space
Minimum margin equal to the cap-height of the wordmark, on all four sides. The sweep counts as part of the lockup for clear-space purposes.
Minimum size
14px wordmark, 40px sweep. Below this, drop the sweep and use the wordmark alone — see "Reductions" below.
Background
Ink (#050505) preferred for ceremonial moments (preloader, hero, footer). Cream (#FAF7F2) for everyday use. White cards inside cream sections are fine. Photos and saturated colors are not.
Animation
When animated: letters fade + lift in stagger (50–80ms each, 0.6s duration, ease-out-quint). The sweep arrives last, expanding from 0 to full width at 0.8s with the same ease.

Reductions & companion marks.

At sizes where the sweep can no longer be drawn cleanly (~12px and below), or where vertical space is constrained (favicon, nav, app chrome), use one of the approved reductions. The reductions defer to the full lockup; they do not replace it.

Never.

  • ✕Never set the wordmark in a sans-serif. Inter is for clerical work; the brand is editorial.
  • ✕Never use gold on a coloured or photographic background. Ink or cream only.
  • ✕Never bold or italicise the full wordmark. The italic cut belongs to single emphasis words inside sentences, not to the lockup.
  • ✕Never drop the sweep at sizes ≥ 24px. The lockup is the gesture; without the sweep, it's a string of letters.
  • ✕Never tighten the letter-spacing below 0.18em. The openness is the brand's posture.
  • ✕Never enclose the lockup in a box, badge, or circular avatar. The lockup is the badge.
  • ✕Never animate the sweep on a loop. It is a single arrival gesture. After the page settles, the sweep is still.

Why it works.

Hierarchy
Three plans, but only two visual weights: Free is restrained, Studio+ is the hero, Enterprise is a different conversation entirely (different ground, different ask). No "starter / pro / business / scale" matrix.
Section header
Standard editorial pattern — small gold kicker (Pricing), serif h2 with one italic gold word (pricing.), one-line subhead. The kicker is the section's name, not a tagline.
Cream + hairline
The section sits on cream. The hairline gold-fade at the top (divider-gold-center) is the only ornament. No background gradient. No floating shapes.
Cards
10px radius. 1px hairline border (rgba(0,0,0,0.06)). Shadow is whisper-thin (0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)). Studio+ promotes to 1.5px gold border and a wide, gold-tinted glow.
Recommended tab
Solid gold tab anchored at top-left of the featured card, 9px uppercase letters, 0.22em tracking, velvet ink text. Single visual hierarchy nudge — no "MOST POPULAR" sparkles.
Price typography
Numerals set in Noto Serif Display ($9.99) at 36–40px. Unit (/month) in 13px sans, muted. Price changes animate with a 6px y-fade swap (220ms, ease-out-quint).
Toggle
Pill switch, 44×26px, cream-dark off-state, gold on-state, white knob. Active side label is ink; inactive is muted. "Save ~20%" pill appears with a soft scale-in only when yearly is on.
CTAs
Free uses a quiet outlined button (cream-dark border). Studio+ uses the gold button — same one used everywhere else as the brand's primary action. Enterprise uses a tinted-gold button on velvet (rgba gold bg + rgba gold border).
Limit honesty
The Free card carries a small, candidly-worded limit note ("agency applications are capped at 3 per month") inside a soft cream-warm callout. This is the trust signal that distinguishes Pholio from churn-funnel SaaS.
Enterprise as essay
Full-width velvet card below the grid. No number — a serif sentence (Let's talk.) in place of the price. Single divider line between left column (pitch) and right column (4 bullets). This is how Pholio talks to agencies.
Tax/legal line
11–12px sans, muted, centered. One sentence. Mentions USD and that plans renew. This is non-optional — it's the section's "signed and dated" line.
Pattern Use
This pattern is also the canonical shape for: the Studio+ upgrade dialog inside the product, the agency-tier comparison in sales materials, and any "before/after" plan view. When in doubt, copy this header → toggle → grid-of-two → wide-velvet → fine-print rhythm exactly.
Chapter III — The Pill System

Four tiers,
four voices.

Pholio uses pills wherever a tier or status needs to be named in passing — a profile header, a price card, an account dropdown, a nav, an upgrade hint, an agency dashboard row. Pills are small, branded, recurring; if we let them drift, the product becomes a colour-coded mess. The four tiers below are the only ones we draw.

On Paper Free Studio+ Ultra Enterprise
On Ink Free Studio+ Ultra Enterprise

Four voices, in order.

01 Free Quiet · Hairline outline · Ink text

Free is restraint. It is the absence of a brand mark, not the presence of a lower one. The pill is transparent, with a thin ink hairline border and muted text. It should read as "default state," not as "you are missing something."

Background
transparent
Text
rgba(26,26,26,0.62) on cream · rgba(255,255,255,0.55) on ink
Border
1px solid rgba(26,26,26,0.16) · invert on ink
Hover
Text darkens to #1A1A1A; border to rgba(26,26,26,0.32). No fill change.
02 Studio+ Hero · Solid gold · Velvet text

Studio+ is the primary tier. The pill is solid gold (#C9A55A) with velvet ink text. This is the only pill in the system that fills. It carries the same chromatic weight as the gold CTA button — because upgrading to Studio+ is the same action everywhere.

Background
#C9A55A (Gold)
Text
#050505 (Ink) — never white, never gold-dark
Border
Same as background — no visible border
Hover
Background shifts to #D4BC8A (Gold Light). No scale. No shadow lift.
In context
Used once per surface. If a screen names "Studio+" three times, only the most authoritative instance pills it.
03 Ultra Exclusive · Velvet fill · Italic serif gold

Ultra sits above Studio+ — the future tier for talent who command the room. It cannot out-gold Studio+ (that would muddy the hierarchy), so it does the opposite: it inverts. Velvet ink fill, italic serif label, gold text, gold hairline border. It is the only pill that uses Noto Serif Display italic and the only one whose label is not all-caps. It reads like a notation in a private ledger.

Background
#050505 on cream · rgba(255,255,255,0.04) on ink (preserves contrast)
Text
#C8A96E (Gold Warm), italic serif, mixed-case
Border
1px solid rgba(201,165,90,0.55)
Hover
Text + border lift to #D4BC8A. Background unchanged.
Typography
Noto Serif Display italic 400 · 12px (lg: 13.5px) · letter-spacing 0.08em · NOT uppercase. This is the only pill that breaks the all-caps convention — intentionally.
04 Enterprise Institutional · Ink outline · Gold dot prefix

Enterprise is for agencies, not talent. The pill is institutional: ink outline on transparent, ink text, with a small gold dot prefix that signals "this is a different conversation." It is the visual equivalent of a business card rather than a price tag.

Background
transparent
Text
#1A1A1A on cream · rgba(255,255,255,0.92) on ink
Border
1px solid #050505 on cream · rgba(255,255,255,0.55) on ink
Dot prefix
5px circle, gold (#C9A55A), inset before the label with 6px gap. The dot is required — it is what distinguishes Enterprise from a generic outline pill.
Hover
Background fills to ink, text becomes Gold Warm (#C8A96E). One of only two pills with a fill-on-hover (the other is Free); both signal "click to learn more."

When each pill appears.

Pills are tier names made visible. They appear wherever a tier is being declared — not described. A button that says "Upgrade to Studio+" doesn't need a pill; a profile row that's already a Studio+ member does.

Never.

  • ✕Never invent a fifth pill. "Starter", "Plus", "Premium", "Pro" — these don't exist in Pholio. The four tiers are the four tiers. Use existing copy for sub-states.
  • ✕Never apply two pills to one entity. A talent is on exactly one tier. If you need to show plan history, use a timeline, not a pill stack.
  • ✕Never re-colour Studio+. No dark Studio+, no white Studio+, no gradient Studio+. It is gold. Always.
  • ✕Never give Ultra a fill. Ultra's quietness is the point. A filled Ultra reads as a louder Studio+ and ruins the ladder.
  • ✕Never drop the Enterprise dot. Without the gold dot, the Enterprise pill reads as a generic "outline tag" and loses its tier-meaning.
  • ✕Never use status pills (Verified, New, Pending) in the same row as tier pills. If both are required, separate them with a hairline or use a different surface element (a small italic gold word in body copy is preferred).
  • ✕Never animate a pill. No pulse, no shimmer. Tier is identity. Identity doesn't blink.
