## 2. Flow map

Single-page application (no multi-page wizard, no login/account gate). Sequence:

1. Navigate to `https://www.wilhelmina.com/become-a-model`.
2. **Imposter-warning modal** covers the page (OBSERVED, [Ev. 3]) — must click "CONTINUE" to dismiss. This is a real gate: the form fields exist in the DOM underneath but the modal captures focus/visibility.
3. A cookie-consent banner ("This site uses cookies to provide web functionality and performance measurement." / "Got it") is also present and persists at the bottom until dismissed; it does not block form interaction.
4. The **entire 10-section form renders on one page**, in this visible order (headed "1." through "10."), with **conditional sections that only appear once a division is picked**:
   - §1 CHOOSE A CITY* (checkboxes, always visible)
   - §2 WHAT IS YOUR NAME?* (always visible)
   - §3 WHEN WERE YOU BORN?* (always visible)
   - §4 WHERE DO YOU CURRENTLY LIVE?* (always visible)
   - §5 WHAT IS THE BEST WAY TO CONTACT YOU?* (always visible)
   - §6 WHICH DIVISION WOULD YOU LIKE TO JOIN?* — WOMEN / MEN toggle buttons, always visible, plus two US-only interest checkboxes (see §3, §6 below) that are conditionally shown/hidden by city selection (see below).
   - §7 WHAT ARE YOUR MEASUREMENTS?*, §8 UPLOAD YOUR BEST PHOTOS*, §9 HOW DO WE FIND YOU ON SOCIAL MEDIA?, §10 ANYTHING YOU'D LIKE TO SHARE? — **these four sections, plus the RESET FORM / SUBMIT buttons, do not exist in the DOM at all until WOMEN or MEN is clicked** (OBSERVED: `document.querySelector('button[type=submit]')` returns `null` before a division is picked, confirmed live [Ev. 12]). Once a division is picked, the measurement field set differs by division (§3 below).
5. Filling every visible field, including 4/4 photo uploads, client-side (never submitted) — **the Submit button carries a hardcoded `disabled` attribute and stayed disabled through every state tested**, including full valid data on all 20 text/measurement fields plus all 4 photo slots filled (OBSERVED, live-verified this session [Ev. 13]). The DOM contains a hidden `<textarea name="g-recaptcha-response">` with an empty value, and `window.grecaptcha` (Google reCAPTCHA v2 API, loaded via `https://www.google.com/recaptcha/api.js?onload=onloadcallback&render=explicit`) is present but **no visible reCAPTCHA widget (checkbox or badge) ever rendered on screen**, even after full-page scroll to the submit button ([Ev. 13], [Ev. 14]).

7. No login, account creation, or multi-page redirect exists anywhere in this flow.

## 7. Minors & guardians

This is the highest-priority accuracy item and it contains a real, evidenced contradiction:

- The **only** published minor-handling text anywhere on the entire flow, verbatim, appears directly under the birthdate field: *"NOTE: Minors must ask parent/legal guardian's authorization."* [Ev. 4, Ev. 16] — appears identically regardless of city or division selected.
- **There is no guardian name field, no guardian email/phone field, no guardian e-signature/consent checkbox, and no separate "apply as a minor" flow anywhere in the DOM.** The note is the entirety of the minor-handling mechanism as written.
- Simultaneously, the date-of-birth `<input type="date">` carries a hard `max="2008-01-01"` that the browser enforces via native validity (`rangeOverflow`), and the Submit button was observed to remain disabled with any post-2008-01-01 date entered even when every other field (including all 4 photos) was valid and complete [Ev. 16]. As of this capture (2026-08-19), that cutoff **functionally requires the applicant to be at least ~18 years and 7 months old** — i.e. **the form's own date picker technically prevents anyone the guardian-authorization note is addressed to (a minor) from ever completing a valid submission.** Whether this `max` value is a static hardcoded constant (evidence suggests yes: `2008-01-01` is a round date, not `today − 18y` which would land on `2008-08-19`) or a rarely-updated computed value could not be determined from the client side alone.
- **Conclusion**: Wilhelmina publishes guardian-consent language implying minors can apply, but provides no guardian-consent mechanism and the form's own client-side date validation appears to block any DOB indicating an age under ~18 from ever reaching a submittable state. This is a genuine, evidenced trap for a talent-facing product to warn about (see §10, §11).

## 10. Contradictions & uncertainties

Ranked by how badly each could surprise a talent using Pholio:

1. **[HIGH] Minor-guardian note vs. DOB field's hard cutoff.** The form tells minors to get "parent/legal guardian's authorization" (implying they can and should apply), but the date-of-birth field's `max="2008-01-01"` constraint technically blocks entry of any birthdate indicating an age under ~18 (as of 2026-08-19) from ever validating, and the Submit button stayed disabled with such a date in every test performed this session, even with every other field and all 4 photos filled. A minor applicant following the "ask your guardian" instruction may not even be able to get the form into a submittable state. See §7.
2. **[HIGH] Marketing framing vs. actual form scope.** The LANE-BRIEF's expected framing — "female, male, curve, fitness, and non-binary talent ages 16 and up... no professional experience required" — **could not be found published anywhere on wilhelmina.com** in this session (checked: home page meta description/body, `/our-legacy` About Us page, `/become-a-model` page text, targeted keyword search for "curve", "non-binary", "16 and up", "no professional experience" across all fetched pages — zero verbatim matches [Ev. 4, Ev. 22]). Wilhelmina's actual "Our Legacy" copy says only *"women, men and fitness models, as well as entertainers and musicians"* — no curve, no non-binary, no stated age floor. The application form itself offers only a binary WOMEN/MEN toggle plus two US-only interest checkboxes (Sports+Fitness, WillyMoves) — **no Curve option, no non-binary option, anywhere in the form**, despite Curve being a real, currently-active division with its own roster page (`/new-york/curve/main`, confirmed live [Ev. 25]). A talent expecting to self-identify as Curve or non-binary on this form cannot do so. Flag this divergence prominently; the phrase attributed to Wilhelmina in the assignment brief does not match any first-party text found.
3. **[MEDIUM] File size / count limits for photo uploads are entirely unpublished.** No text anywhere states a max file size, and testing real-sized files risked an actual upload, which is prohibited — so this is a genuine unknown, not a "no limit" finding.
4. **[MEDIUM] reCAPTCHA gate makes the true final-validation rules unverifiable.** The Submit button stayed disabled in every state tested, including a fully-valid, fully-photographed dry run, because of an un-obtainable `g-recaptcha-response` token. It is unknown whether, once reCAPTCHA is solved, any *additional* server-side validation would reject the submission (e.g. real file-size enforcement, email-format checks, phone-format checks). Everything documented in §3 about "what makes Submit enable" is therefore a lower bound, not a complete picture.

6. **[LOW] Multi-city selection is allowed but its effect on downstream state/routing is unknown.** Checking Miami + London simultaneously left both checked with no error and no visible extra instruction change (other than the US-only checkboxes toggling per the presence of a US city) — but since Submit could never be pressed, it's unknown whether multi-city applications are actually processed differently, deduped, or rejected server-side.

This was not reproduced carefully enough across repeated runs to be certain whether NY is genuinely "sticky"/default-locked or whether this was a test-timing artifact. Flagged UNCERTAIN, not asserted as a real product behavior.
8. **[LOW] Same email address reused across two locations.** `sports+fitness@wilhelmina.com` is listed as both the New York and Los Angeles Sports+Fitness contact on the same Contact Us page — could be intentional (shared regional inbox) or a copy-paste artifact on Wilhelmina's own site. Recorded as-is per the "verbatim, don't silently resolve" rule.
9. **[LOW] No visible client-side error/validation messages anywhere.** A DOM-wide search for validation-style text (matching /required|invalid|must be|please enter a valid/i) returned zero visible elements at any point in testing. The only validation feedback surfaced to a real user is: the browser's native date-picker tooltip (for DOB range violations) and a greyed-out Submit button with no explanation of why. A talent who mis-fills any field would get no on-page guidance as to which field is wrong.

## 11. Draft talent-facing brief (150–300 words)

Wilhelmina's talent application lives entirely on one page (wilhelmina.com/become-a-model) — no account, no multi-step wizard. Before you see the form, dismiss a scam-imposter warning; Wilhelmina is explicit that it will never accept applications by direct email, only through this form.

Have ready: your full name, birthdate, current city/state/country, email and phone, and — once you pick WOMEN or MEN — your measurements. Women: height, bust, cup, waist, hips, shoe, hair color, eye color. Men: height, waist, suit, inseam, shoe, hair color, eye color, collar. If you're a US applicant you can also opt into Sports+Fitness or WillyMoves (these checkboxes disappear if you only select London). You'll need exactly 4 photos (JPEG/PNG/WebP/AVIF/TIFF/HEIC accepted, one per slot) — Wilhelmina gives no written instructions on what to wear or how to shoot them; the only guidance is 4 example thumbnails showing a plain background, minimal makeup, progressing from a close-up face shot to a full-body shot. Instagram/TikTok and a free-text notes box are optional.

Two traps worth knowing: the birthdate field technically won't accept a date suggesting you're under about 18, even though the form separately tells minors to get a guardian's authorization — the agency doesn't explain this gap. And there is no visible error messaging anywhere; if the Submit button stays greyed out, the form won't tell you why. Wilhelmina says it only follows up if interested — no stated timeline, no confirmation of receipt.
