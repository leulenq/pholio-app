# Round 2: Trust & Safety (trust)

Research note: the shared web-search budget ran out during this round. Fetching primary sources still worked, but I could not run the roster-tool search (Kavyar, StudioBinder, Reddit). Where I have no source, I say so.

## 1. Attack on A (the Issuer Desk): it sells "verified" to the people who most need to fake it
- **The market selects the wrong customers.** The organizers most eager to pay for a published, branded credit page are the pay-to-walk operators: $625 for "the runway walk" ([FACT](https://form.jotform.com/260485991034159)), plus a crowded field of non-CFDA "NYFW" platforms ([FACT](https://www.buzzfeed.com/hannahmarder/are-fashion-week-models-paid)). [BET] Under A, every rejected issuer is lost revenue. Every company whose revenue comes from the parties it certifies eventually loosens its standards. That is the Twitter Blue path ([FACT](https://www.axios.com/2023/04/20/twitter-blue-checkmarks-verifications-removed)).
- **A credit issued to a name in a form is not issued to a person.** The founder's version has profiles "auto-created and claimed with an emailed link". So "Issued by FWB" attaches to whoever controls the inbox the organizer typed in. One typo hands someone else a person's runway history. With no identity check at claim, ISSUED proves the organizer's spreadsheet, not the human.
- **The organizer holds the most sensitive data in the system.** Open-call intake brings in hundreds of applicants' faces, bodies, ages and contact details, including minors: FWB castings take people as young as 17, and FWB has run a kids' show (R4). A Google Form is a liability FWB carries. Under A, Pholio becomes the processor that holds the breach.

## 2. Attacks on named claims
- **Founder:** "the ISSUED credit costs FWB one click. Nobody has to maintain anything." A one-click bulk issue is exactly the bad-CSV blast radius. One click needs one named, ID-verified signatory plus a 7-day private dispute window. Otherwise it is a "fake top-billed credits" engine, like IMDb's *KGF* profiles ([FACT](https://qz.com/imposters-game-bollywood-imdb-pages-to-fool-google-1849393865)).
- **Money:** "Evidence Pack … for … O-1B petitions." [FACT](https://meritdraft.com/decisions/o-1b-dismissed-fashion-model-2018-03-30.html) A model's O-1B was revoked over a falsified testimonial letter. Once Pholio sells a dossier for federal filings, our attestation becomes a material statement to USCIS. That is the worst place to be wrong. Cut it.
- **Creative:** "Pholio vouches for the team list and carries no liability." Vouching *is* liability, and the two halves of that sentence contradict each other.
- **Hirer**, answering your question: who is exposed when an ISSUED credit is wrong? [ASSUMPTION, legal reading] The issuer is the speaker. Pholio keeps Section 230 cover only if it does not author the credit ([FACT](https://www.minclaw.com/legal-resource-center/what-is-section-230-of-the-communication-decency-act-cda/)). Your brand is exposed only if it is named. So the client field must be off by default for commercial work.

## 3. Red-team of C (capture-verified digitals)
- **Biometric law.** Liveness and face matching create face templates. Illinois BIPA carries $1,000 per negligent violation and $5,000 per intentional or reckless one, with a private right of action and no injury required (*Rosenbach*). Settlements include Facebook ($650M over face scans of photos) ([FACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometric_Information_Privacy_Act)). The creatives we can reach include out-of-state models. [ASSUMPTION] Texas and Washington have biometric statutes too.
- **The Fashion Workers Act.** New York's FWA (signed Dec 2024, effective June 2025) requires "clear, conspicuous and separate written consent" before a model's digital replica is altered with AI ([FACT](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2023/S9832); [FACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Alliance)). [BET] An ID-matched, recent, unretouched, full-length library of young models is the best replica-training set anyone could ask for. C builds the asset that scrapers and bad "clients" want most.
- **Deepfake injection.** iProov reported a 704% rise in face-swap attacks in H1 2023. Sensity found bank liveness checks "easily cheated" ([FACT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liveness_test)). [ASSUMPTION] A *web* capture flow cannot prove the camera feed is not a virtual camera. Any "unretouched, taken 9 days ago" label needs a native app with device attestation, which is not a v1 scope.
- **Minors.** C makes age band a headline field. That means ID-checking 16–17-year-olds, which raises guardian consent, ID retention and the question of what we hold. A "current digitals" feed of minors is a grooming catalogue. Minors must be excluded from C entirely.

## 4. Red-team of B (team sheets as a tool for fake shoots)
- The FTC says modeling scams run on fake shoots and invented brand affiliations ([FACT](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/modeling-scams)). B gives a scammer a Pholio-branded "production" naming a fake magazine, invites by link with no account, and a public page that says "Confirmed by the team", where the team is all sock puppets.
- Pull letters are already sold as credentials ([FACT](https://flanellemag.com/?p=16294)). A Pholio-generated pull letter is a sample-theft tool, because showrooms release garments on its strength.
- Sheets publish who will be where and when. [GAP] I have no incident data on fake-photographer lures; the search budget ran out.

## 6. Revision
1. **Cut MUTUAL from v1.** I concede this to founder and competition. The rename was not enough.
2. **Claiming an ISSUED credit requires an ID check of the named person,** not an emailed link. Pholio stores no face templates: a vendor runs the check and keeps the data, under a written retention schedule.
3. **B is allowed only for ID-verified leads.** No pull-letter generation. Future-dated sheets stay private.
4. **C is limited to adults, with no face search across the database, ever.**
5. **Paying for the tool is never a right to issue credits** (money's rule zero). Issuer vetting is run by someone with no revenue target.

**Question:** founder, will you put "issuers rejected per quarter" next to MRR on the board deck? If not, A becomes a laundering business.
