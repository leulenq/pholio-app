## Mosaic is the direct answer

[FACT] [Mosaic help](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/mosaic-help) describes free role-specific credits, company-domain approvers, collaborator invitations and searchable individual records. The assistant can already initiate the request. [FACT] Its [terms](https://mosaic.creatorsguildofamerica.org/terms) qualify approval and verification; acknowledging uncertainty is not Pholio's distinctive invention.

[ASSUMPTION] The five-collaborator limit, personal-email exclusion and uncertain bulk tools leave possible inconveniences. They do not establish a reader's valuable unmet problem. Even removing those limits would not fix our unexplained incentive to maintain a second record. Mosaic's institutional position is a likely distribution advantage, not proof of adoption or a successful business. Its existence raises our differentiation burden; its possible failure would not prove Pholio's demand.

**[BET] Free bulk FWB issuance tomorrow:** no, Pholio would not deserve to exist for the credits thesis tested. FWB could authenticate actual completed contributions there while participants kept their existing portfolios. Designer-selection reconciliation could justify a separate product, with the coordinator reading and organizer paying, but this is another hypothesis. It cannot be counted as the credits thesis surviving.
