# Round 3, Growth

## 1. Vote: AMEND

## 2. Fatal flaw: H's loop runs through the one person with the least reason to run it
H has growth come from leads opening productions. The cadence the room actually found is about **one test a month** per photographer ([FACT](https://www.adorama.com/alc/how-to-plan-a-model-portfolio-shoot), via founder R2). Creative's estimate is that about 1 in 5 invitees ever leads a shoot [ASSUMPTION]. The arithmetic: 1 production a month × ~5 invitees × 1/5 who lead × p(that lead opens their own production). With p = 0.15 [ASSUMPTION], each lead produces about 0.15 new leads a month, or about 0.9 over six months. That is subcritical, and the lead's gain over Kavyar plus a group chat is about 10 minutes (creative's concession). **As drafted, H is my round-1 invite loop with a weaker engine. It will need hand-selling forever, which is A again.**

The people who actually want the record are the **members**: the assistant, HMU, manicurist and dresser whose credit gets dropped on the repost (creative R2; [FACT](https://petapixel.com/2017/04/21/copyright-censorship-instagram-marie-claire-stole-photo/)). H gives them no way to start anything.

## 3. Required amendments (replacement text)
1. Replace **"Who opens one: whoever assembles the team…"** with:
   > "Who opens one: **anyone who worked on it.** A member can open a production for a shoot they were on and invite the lead to confirm it. The lead's confirmation is required before anything is public. The lead is the confirmer, not the bottleneck."
2. Replace **"Launch: … Then 15–30 hand-recruited NYC photographers and stylists who lead shoots."** with:
   > "Distribution: every published production has a short credit link ('Full credits: pholio.studio/c/…') that the lead and every member paste into captions, Stories and repost requests. Growth is measured by (a) credit-page visits from non-members and (b) productions opened by non-leads. Seed: FWB shows, then 15–30 NYC shoot leads **and the people on their last three shoots**."
3. Replace **"a paste-ready credit block and a Kavyar-ready export"** with:
   > "a paste-ready credit block formatted for Kavyar's handle fields by copy and paste. No Kavyar integration is promised or required."

## 4. Rebuttals
- **Creative:** *"Models, assistants and makeup artists almost never organize… K is bounded by the share of invitees who lead shoots, maybe 1 in 5."* Conceded for the invite loop. That is exactly why amendment 1 exists. The loop I'm defending is no longer lead → invitee → lead. It is **member-pull** plus **caption circulation**. [BET] The person whose credit disappears on reposts asks the lead to "put it on Pholio," the same way models ask photographers for selects. Every published production then shows the credit link to the audiences of 5–8 accounts, the way Linktree spread through bios rather than through invites.
- **Competition:** *"Those 15 seed producers already credit their teams in Kavyar… otherwise it is double entry."* Kavyar entry happens at *submission*. Plenty of tests are never submitted, and Kavyar keeps no saved teams ([FACT](https://help.kavyar.com/article/p03wnxoixc)). Under amendment 3, the Kavyar step becomes one paste.

## 5. Owned problems
**#1 Frequency.** Accept monthly *leading* as the base rate. The record grows from *appearances*, though, not from leading: a stylist leads one shoot but is on several [ASSUMPTION, which the test below checks]. Retention should be measured monthly, not weekly. Cut the weekly north star; the new one is *published productions per month with at least 4 confirmed members*.

**#2 Kavyar.** Pholio sits **before** Kavyar (casting and assembly) and **after** it (publication and reposts). Kavyar owns only the upload moment. The export is clipboard text, so it needs no goodwill from Kavyar. If Kavyar adds saved teams and public credit pages, H loses the photographer and keeps only member-pull.

## 6. The 14-day test, no code
Concierge credit pages. Take **10 NYC shoots published in the last 30 days**: indie-mag editorials and tests. For each, the founders hand-build a full-team credit page (Carrd or Notion, every role) and DM *every team member*, not just the lead. Measure:
- (a) the share of members who post or link it;
- (b) how many members, unprompted, ask for one for a *different* shoot;
- (c) for each member, how many productions they appeared on in the last 30 days.

- **Accept:** at least 30% share the link, and 3 or more unprompted requests.
- **Reject H as a network:** under 10% share and zero requests. A is all that's left.
