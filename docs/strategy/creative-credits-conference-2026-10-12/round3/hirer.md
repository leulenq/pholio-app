# Round 3: HIRER

## 1. Vote: AMEND

## 2. The fatal flaw: H is only credible for the work nobody pays for
- H publishes only *after public release*, lets "the lead **or** client" set the embargo, and never involves brands.
- That makes the public record mostly test shoots, editorials and FWB shows. That is the low-stakes tier.
- The credits that would make a creative credible to me are paid commercial work, campaigns and music videos. These are exactly the credits under NDA, embargo or exclusivity ([FACT](https://www.freelancermap.com/blog/can-i-share-nda-protected-work-on-my-portfolio-tips-and-advice/); [FACT](https://www.iphotography.com/blog/business-photography-industry-legal-terms-explained/)).
- They are also often left uncredited on purpose for contract reasons ([FACT](https://thefader.com/2014/09/23/alicia-keyes-stylists-legal-fracas)). R4 Against #3: "the highest-value credits … are the least likely to be issued."
- Under H as written, those credits either sit in a vault forever, or the *lead* chooses the date and names my brand. That is a breach I would answer with a takedown, and it gives every brand legal team a reason to ban crews from Pholio.
- The result is a record that is credible but trivial, or substantial but unsafe.
- The vault needs a third state: **confirmed and shareable privately, client undisclosed.**

## 3. Required amendments (exact replacement text)

**A1.** Replace "The lead or client controls the embargo date." with:
> "No client or brand is ever named publicly unless a public URL of the released work is attached. The client field is off by default for commercial work. Any named client can remove its name on request without a dispute, and the credit then reads 'undisclosed client'. Leads cannot set a client's release date."

**A2.** Add to Outputs:
> "Private proof link: any confirmed credit, including embargoed or NDA work, can be shared by its holder with one named recipient as an expiring link. The link shows role, month, production type ('commercial, undisclosed client') and the names of the confirming teammates. It is never indexed and never names the client."

**A3.** Replace "Organizer and agency intake workspaces pay later." with:
> "Second paid line, from month 9: agency and casting intake workspaces ($49–99/mo) that receive applications as Pholio pages with dated digitals and submission snapshots. Hirers never pay to search or to contact anyone."

On A3, money's round-2 point that "'free always' throws away the one revenue line with evidence" is correct. I concede filtered intake. I hold the line on search and contact.

## 4. Rebuttal
VC: *"The only durable part of C is capture-dated digitals, a model-only feature that Meta Verified or Casting Networks could ship in one quarter."*
- I never claimed identity as the moat. Pholio should *buy* identity from a vendor at about $1 a check ([FACT](https://withpersona.com/pricing)), which also answers trust's BIPA point, since the vendor holds the templates.
- What doesn't commoditize is *where the file is consumed*: the frozen, consented snapshot inside an intake I already have to process.
- Meta Verified proves "a paying adult with this name." It tells me nothing about whether this person showed up on a set. A2 does.

Founder: *"ID plus liveness in front of a free, exposure-tier open call adds friction."*
- Agreed, so put it at *claim and confirm*, not at registration.
- I withdraw "mandatory at intake" from round 2.

## 5. Resolving #4: what "credible" buys the creative, concretely
Hirers don't search. They **check a link they were sent, at a referral moment**.
- [FACT] Fashion is 82% contacts ([Bectu](https://bectu.org.uk/news/three-quarters-of-creative-industries-jobs-go-to-friends-and-connections-reveals-bectus-bectu-survey)). Casting directors "talk to each other" and check claims ([Backstage](https://www.backstage.com/advice-for-actors/backstage-experts/why-actors-shouldnt-lie-their-resumes/)). The check is a phone call to someone I trust.
- What I read on a credit is **the confirmers' names, not the tier**. "Confirmed by [a DP I've booked]" is a referral I can verify with one text.
- [ASSUMPTION] That second-degree check is the only verification behaviour I actually perform.

The concrete moments, in order of frequency:
1. **A soft referral.** "My friend says use this MUA." I open the link and look for a name I know.
2. **An agency or open-call application.** The snapshot goes into intake (A3).
3. **A cold DM pitch to a producer.** A link with three confirmed paid jobs (A2) beats a grid of tests.
4. **A magazine submission** (Kavyar export).
5. **O-1B or grant evidence.** This should be a free export that is not attested, per trust's USCIS warning.

Design consequence: every confirmer's name links to their own page. Without that, "Confirmed by" is a caption.

## 6. A cheap test in 14 days, no code
- Mock up 10 Pholio pages in Figma for real NYC crew members, each with 3–5 credits. Include one A2-style "commercial, undisclosed client" credit with named confirmers.
- Show each page cold to 10 hirers from the founders' network: producers, agency bookers and casting.
- Ask three questions:
  1. Would you shortlist this person?
  2. What would you do to check?
  3. Would your legal team object to A2?
- **Vote change:**
  - If ≥5 of 10 hirers name a specific confirmer they'd contact, and ≤2 flag A2 as a legal problem, I move to ACCEPT.
  - If fewer than 3 read the confirmers at all, I vote REJECT on H as a "credible" record. It would then be a creative's admin tool, and the founders should say so.
