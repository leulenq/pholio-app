# Moderator draft consensus v1 (for Round 3 attack)

Synthesized from all Round 2 files. The near-unanimous 41/60 vote for this hybrid is itself a warning sign: the room moved from one consensus (A) to another (H) in a single round.

## Thesis H: "The Production Record"
**Pholio is where a creative production's team is recorded, and the creative's portfolio is the accumulated record of the productions they were confirmed on.**

- **Core object: the Production.** It can be a test shoot, an editorial, a lookbook, a music video or a runway show.
- **Who opens one:** whoever assembles the team, typically a photographer, stylist or producer, or an organizer like FWB for each show.
- **How roles get confirmed:** the lead adds members by role, and each member confirms their own role. Nothing is public until (a) every listed member has confirmed or a timeout has passed AND (b) the work is publicly released. The lead or client controls the embargo date.
- **Labels, in plain text:**
  - "Issued by [vetted organization], signed by [named, ID-verified person]" for vetted organizers.
  - "Confirmed by [lead] and [n] team members" for creative-led productions. This is never called "verified".
  - "Added by [self]" for claimed work. Never indexed.
- **"Verified" refers only to a person** (an outside ID check plus handle control). It never refers to a credit.
- **Outputs:**
  - a full-team credit page with no 6-person cap;
  - a paste-ready credit block and a Kavyar-ready export, so Pholio feeds Kavyar rather than competing with it;
  - an embargo vault for unreleased or NDA work;
  - the person's Pholio page, generated from their confirmed productions.
- **Models:** capture-dated digitals ("taken 9 days ago"), adults only, with no face templates stored by Pholio. This is a feature and a reason to return, not the thesis.
- **Cut from v1:**
  - drafting from Instagram;
  - paid search seats for hirers;
  - brands as issuers or confirmers;
  - pull letters;
  - public credits for minors;
  - forward-dated public credits;
  - anything involving money moving between parties.
- **Launch:** FWB runs each show as an issued production (intake through Pholio replaces Google Forms). Then 15–30 hand-recruited NYC photographers and stylists who lead shoots.
- **Money:**
  - Members and the record stay free forever.
  - Lead creatives pay about $12/mo (Pro tools: unlimited productions, embargo vault, submission exports, evidence packs).
  - Organizer and agency intake workspaces pay later.
  - Issuer status is never sold; vetting is kept separate from billing.
- **Kill test before building billing:** 20 interviews plus 10 observed shoots. Then, of 15 seeded shoot leads, at least 5 must open a second production unprompted within 30–45 days, and at least 20% of FWB-credited photographers and stylists must open a non-FWB production within 45 days. If this fails, Pholio is a small issuer-tool business and should not raise venture money.

## Known unresolved problems (the moderator's own attacks)
1. **Frequency of team assembly is unproven.** Nobody found data. If NYC leads run about 1 production a month, the habit is weak.
2. **Kavyar already captures team credits with handles for every editorial submission.** Why would a creative type the roster into Pholio first? "Export to Kavyar" is a promise that depends on Kavyar's goodwill or an unofficial integration.
3. **Contradiction:** trust conceded cutting the MUTUAL tier from v1, but "Confirmed by lead + n members" IS mutual. Which is it?
4. **Hirers.** The room agreed hirers don't search credit records. So what does "credible" buy the creative, concretely, and at which moments? (Candidates: agency applications, grants, O-1B, magazine submissions, a new collaborator vetting you, clients you DM.)
5. **Revenue ceiling.** At $12/mo × 2,000 NYC leads, about $288k/yr. Is this venture-scale anywhere, or a good small business? Be honest.
6. **"Living record."** Has H delivered the founders' goal, or a better Kavyar?
