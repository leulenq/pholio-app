# Round 4 — Creative professional: Mosaic removes our default reason to exist

**[BET] Vote: reject a creative-credit rebuild. Choose one bounded product experiment: reusable portfolios inside a returning-participant casting desk for independent runway organizers.** This is narrower than creative identity. Credits may be an optional completed-work output; they cannot justify the product until a real reader demonstrates additional value.

## Mosaic is the objection, not an inconvenient footnote

[FACT] Mosaic lets a creator initiate a role-specific credit, name collaborators and request authentication from a company-domain approver. Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud addresses are excluded; registration and credit creation are free. Its help separates those credits from guild membership verification: [Mosaic help](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/mosaic-help). An excluded assistant can already initiate recognition there. Pholio cannot claim that allowing non-leads to start records is novel.

[ASSUMPTION] For an assistant on commercial work with an accessible producer at the production company's domain, Mosaic is the first alternative Pholio must beat. A nicer page and another confirmation email do not explain switching. We have no observed Mosaic adoption or approval-completion data, so its existence does not prove demand either.

[FACT] The same help specifies up to five collaborators and a domain-based approver. [ASSUMPTION] Individual test shoots with personal-email leads may fall outside that authentication path. That is a product boundary, not a market. [BET] If a test-shoot crew member cannot use Mosaic, compare Pholio with the actual substitute—Instagram credits, their own portfolio and references—not an artificially empty competitor column.

## Lived workflow beats the room's imagined habit

[FACT] RGG Photo studio manager/producer Gary Winchester Martin's first-person [assistant guide](https://cdn.fstoppers.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/Photo-Assistant-Guide-RGG-PHOTO-Fstoppers.pdf) describes job-based assistant rosters, repeat referrals from performance on set, and contacting previous photographers when assessing assistants. It distinguishes first assistants, digital techs and general assistants by responsibilities. This older practitioner's guide supports a reference-check workflow; it supplies no current NYC usage rate.

[FACT] Casting director Paul Russell recounts personally catching a false lead-role claim during an open call because he directed that production: [his account](https://www.backstage.com/magazine/article/actors-lie-resumes-13656/). This is one acting example, not evidence that model bookers routinely verify credits. [ASSUMPTION] A work record can help some checks, but its relevance depends on the role: an assistant's equipment competence, a makeup artist's visible execution, a model's current appearance and an influencer's audience are different selection questions.

## Named attacks and my own retreat

**Founder:** [ASSUMPTION] Your Round 4 casting desk has an actual reader, but it abandons the assistant who supposedly motivated the pivot. Say that plainly. FWB model intake will not automatically acquire backstage crews, musicians or artists. [BET] Choose runway applicants as the initial user; any expansion requires its own observed workflow.

**VC:** [ASSUMPTION] A returning-talent file may save organizer effort, but a next-season invite is not frequent creative engagement. A model opening a compulsory form twice proves compliance, not preference. [BET] Measure whether applicants voluntarily reuse their portfolio for a second actual casting and whether an organizer purchases reuse. Do not call the season-to-season file a universal identity network.

**Growth:** [ASSUMPTION] “The lead is the confirmer, not the bottleneck” contradicts requiring that lead's confirmation before publication. An assistant with less power can create a request but still cannot make the photographer answer. Caption clicks likewise demonstrate attention, not hiring or reference value. Mosaic already supplies that request pattern for organizational work.

**Hirer:** [ASSUMPTION] A named confirmer is useful only when the reader recognizes or actually contacts them. A private link cannot establish permission, attendance or competence. Watch real reference checks instead of asking who someone would text.

**My Round 3 safety thesis:** [ASSUMPTION, withdrawn] I implied confirmed collaborators made unfamiliar photographers safe. Real credits and ID checks can coexist with misconduct. [FACT] Model Alliance's [model-reported research](https://www.modelalliance.org/published) describes workplace privacy and harassment problems among working models. Proven work history is not a conduct guarantee. I also withdraw unsupported claims that The Dots failed because of re-entry and that StudioBinder has no handles field.

## One product, an honest boundary and a falsifier

[BET] The casting coordinator reads submitted portfolio snapshots to shortlist; the organizer pays for reconciliation, designer review and returning-participant invitations. A creative returns for a second genuine casting, not to renew a credibility score. An excluded assistant can request a correction to an optional completed-participation record; this experiment does not build a separate assistant-initiated credit network.

[BET] Observe the existing FWB workflow, then test a priced casting pilot with a separate organizer. Compare applicant reuse and coordinator effort with Forms plus existing portfolios. Separately let willing commercial crew members try free Mosaic. If Mosaic resolves their credit need, recommend it. If organizers decline paid repeat use and applicants get no reuse benefit, reject this product too. No credits pivot should survive merely because we dislike the alternative.
