Variant A: Radar Ripple

  • Aesthetic: Adds a dashed compilation orbit frame rotating slowly around the ripple core.
  • Transitions: Ripple scaling scales up to `2.2x` on a slow ease-out curve, ensuring clean visual expansion.
  • Typography: Centered Playfair Display status lines that dynamically shift as compilers run.

Variant B: Editorial Text Gated

  • Aesthetic: Completely removes shapes, focusing strictly on oversized serif typography.
  • Transitions: Uses a single horizontal 180px hairline loading bar that fills up over time.
  • Typography: Focuses visual weight onto the textual statements ("Structuring roster...", "Polishing card...").

Variant C: Compass Orbit

  • Aesthetic: Astronomical minimal circle with a satellite dot tracking along the circumference.
  • Transitions: Constant circular translation with percentage counters fading inside the boundary.
  • Typography: Light Outfit display font displaying percentage loads (`0%` to `100%`).
