# Round 5 — Monetization: stop the credit thesis; preserve an independent buyer test

**[BET] Vote: AMEND V2. Forced choice: STOP THESIS.** Stop rebuilding around creative identity or authenticated credits. A useful portfolio may survive; the premise that accumulating credits produces a payer does not. This decision does not require Mosaic to succeed commercially.

## Two disagreements

**Competition:** Your rule “a demonstrated failure for one workflow justifies testing that workflow's remedy” is sound, but Mosaic need not fail before we investigate intake. [ASSUMPTION] Its credit product and an organizer's selection reconciliation solve different tasks. Making Mosaic failure the gateway to organizer research would bias us toward finding competitor defects. An organizer showing a consequential coordination problem should qualify an intake test even if Mosaic serves every credit need perfectly.

**Growth:** Your proposed five priced organizer offers in fourteen days gets ahead of identifying their actual failures. [ASSUMPTION] Refusals of an abstract intake bundle would conflate inadequate pain, poor explanation and mistimed events. First observe a specific repeat process and its existing workaround. Then offer the exact remedial outcome for a stated price. Equally, do not wait for FWB's next season to learn anything: an independent organizer with a real dated need provides evidence the founder relationship cannot.

## The strongest attack on me

**Hirer's objection to premature economics is correct:** my event-priced workspace still risks being a feature bundle looking for pain. [BET] Cost the observed workaround before proposing the pilot; do not calculate an imaginary SaaS margin and call that discovery. My fully costed concierge requirement applies once we deliver. It protects against a different mistake: selling hours of founder attention and interpreting the sale as software demand. Counting labor is necessary; it cannot manufacture a valuable task.

## Free bulk Mosaic tomorrow

[FACT] [Mosaic help](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/mosaic-help) already makes credits free; $99 annual membership buys a broader guild bundle. [BET] If Mosaic offered free bulk FWB issuance tomorrow, Pholio would have no established reason to exist for the tested credit thesis. The possible independent job remains reviewing portfolio submissions, reconciling designer selections and chasing acceptances. Reader: casting coordinator and authorized designers. Possible payer: the organizer. Their willingness to purchase is unknown. No justification comes from being the interface that forwards names into Mosaic.

## Alternative, unknowns and falsifier

[BET] Strongest alternative: use Mosaic in an uncommitted attribution trial and run no new product. Do that if authorized users need records; do not turn successful claims into evidence for Pholio revenue. Partnership stays conditional on supported capabilities and terms.

[ASSUMPTION] Unknowns include organizer pain, purchasing authority, event frequency, current costs, independent acquisition and repeat willingness. [BET] Observe one real independent casting, document a consequential avoidable failure, then offer its specific remedy as a priced pilot. Proceed only if that buyer purchases; assess paid reuse when another eligible event occurs. Log refusals and fully costed delivery. A purchased remedy with improving delivery economics flips me toward BUILD NARROW. Absent demonstrable pain or purchase, retain STOP THESIS. Even success establishes an intake experiment, not a universal identity graph or venture outcome.
