# Round 2: Devil's advocate

Research note: the session's web-search budget was used up before I could run new searches. The roster-tool question (Kavyar, StudioBinder, Sheets, Reddit) is still **unresolved by evidence**, and I flag every claim below that depends on it.

## 1. Attack on Candidate A (the Organizer/Issuer Desk)

A is what a room produces when it is scared of the founders' goal. It swaps "a living record of a creative's actual work" for "event SaaS that prints a page twice a year." It fails on its own terms in three ways.

**It is too episodic to be "living."** FWB runs 2 seasons plus Queens [FACT](https://www.fashionweekbrooklyn.com/schedule), [FACT](https://qns.com/2026/07/queens-center-launch-fashion-week-queens/). A model who walks once gets one line in six months. Growth said it in round 1: "a model who walked in October gets nothing from Pholio until April." Under A, the record for 95% of creatives is one or two ISSUED rows a year with long gaps between them. That is a certificate drawer, not a living record [ASSUMPTION].

**The customer has no money, and that is clear from the research.**
- FWB's founder "reportedly sold his condo to self-fund it" [FACT](https://www.bkreader.com/featured-news/where-brooklyn-at-fashion-week-brooklyn-highlights-hip-hop-culture-in-bk-the-metaverse-and-beyond-6831994).
- Its designer showcase offers "exposure," not stipends [FACT](https://brooklynmadestore.com/pages/brooklyn-fashion-show-2026).
- Indie magazines just lost Kavyar's free tier [FACT](https://help.kavyar.com/article/a6pzp4ugix-publisher-plans-are-changing-what-you-need-to-know). People squeezed off a free tier tend to go back to free (Google Forms, email), not to a new vendor [ASSUMPTION].

A's payer is real, but it is the poorest buyer in the ecosystem.

**Credits as exhaust are credits nobody reads.** A publishes show pages, but nobody has named a reader:
- No hirer in R4 asks for verified credits [FACT of absence, R4].
- Mosaic already has "Authenticated by [org]" and shows no buyer pull [FACT](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/credits).
- So A delivers an output (a credit page) to an audience (hirers) that the same five delegates just argued does not exist. They killed search seats for lack of a reader and then kept the page that needs one.

**What A is in reality** is Kavyar for runway. Competition concedes "Kavyar can add 'Published in X' credits in weeks," which means the incumbent for the magazine half of A already exists and already charges creatives too [FACT](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1510274904).

## 2. Attacks on named delegates

**Money:** "$20 per designer slot per event with a $250 minimum, so FWB's ~40 slots come to about $800 a season."
- That is about $1,600 a year from the anchor customer.
- $1M ARR at that price needs roughly 600 FWB-sized organizers. Nobody has named five (vc and money both asked; nobody answered).
- Money's own Payer 3 depends on "≥200 distinct hirer accounts searching weekly." That revives the search-seat idea that money called "the claim most wrong." Money is betting the company's later revenue on the behaviour it just declared nonexistent.

**Founder:** kill criterion "≥70% of FWB registrations come through Pholio."
- This passes by decree. If FWB swaps its Google Form link for ours, it is 100% on day one.
- It measures FWB's compliance, not creatives' pull.
- The only criterion that tests the thesis is "≥1 independent organizer within 90 days," and A has no mechanism that produces it except founder sales calls.

**Competition:** "Only ISSUED rides on someone else's mandatory work."
- The organizer's work is mandatory only at event cadence.
- The shoot is also mandatory work. Someone has to assemble the team, and per Kavyar someone has to credit the whole team at submission [FACT](https://help.kavyar.com/article/p03wnxoixc).
- It happens weekly for working photographers and stylists [ASSUMPTION: still unverified; see the research note].
- Competition picked the least frequent mandatory job and called it the only one.

**VC:** "the scale story is the … Talent Systems / Casting Networks path."
- Talent Systems got its scale from union and commercial casting, a CSA partnership and a RedBird-funded roll-up [FACT](https://www.castingnetworks.com/?p=100593).
- Nothing in that path starts from a Brooklyn nonprofit's Google Form.
- If VC's own test (2) is likely to fail, A is a tool business that VC himself said should not raise.

## 3. Attack on my own Candidate C
- **The demand evidence is thin.** It rests on one anonymous casting quote plus the norm that agencies ask for digitals [FACT, anecdotal](https://theplayground.com/ai-headshots-vs-real-photography-casting-directors-decide/). A mismatch is caught for free at the go-see. Hirers already have a verification step: meeting the person.
- **The "Pholio as signing origin" idea is fragile.** Phone makers are adding C2PA signing to native camera apps [ASSUMPTION: I believe recent Pixel and Galaxy flagships do; I could not verify this round]. Meta Verified and CLEAR already sell ID [FACT](https://appleinsider.com/articles/23/02/19/meta-verified-is-mark-zuckerbergs-1199-competitor-to-twitter-blue).
- **Liveness friction and no peacetime use.** Talent opens C only when applying, and stylists, photographers and HMU artists barely need it.
- **C abandons the founders' goal even more than A does.** It is about the person, not their work.
- Verdict on C: it is a strong feature, a weak thesis.

**Hybrid H: "The record is written where the work is assembled."**
- **Core object:** B's team sheet, which any shoot lead opens.
- **Distribution:** A becomes a launch channel, not the business. FWB is simply the biggest team sheet, and its organizer desk is free.
- **Identity:** C shrinks to a person-level fact. "ID-checked" plus dated digitals are required only to *confirm* other people's roles, which is trust's rule.
- **Revenue:** leads who run sheets weekly pay for tools: embargo vault, pull-letter drafts, branded credit pages and roster history. They never pay for visibility.
- **Defensibility:** comes from the history across many sheets (who keeps working with whom, by role), which Instagram's 6-person collab cap cannot hold.

## 5. Revised position
- **Changed:** I drop C as the thesis. It is a feature and a trust rule, not the product. My round-1 "Pholio as C2PA origin" claim was overreach.
- **Held:** a record nobody reads has no value, and A builds the record the slowest and most rarely. I also hold that the room has not answered my round-1 question: name a hirer who lost money over an unverifiable credit. Since nobody has, the record has to earn its keep with the people who *create* it (shoot leads), not the people who would *read* it.
- **Kill test for H (taken from growth):** fewer than 5 of 15 hand-recruited NYC shoot leads open a second sheet unprompted within 30 days means B and H die, and A wins by default as an honest small tool business that does not raise venture money. That test must run **before** anyone builds an organizer billing page.
