# Round 1: Competition (opening position)

## 1. Verdict
The "living credible record" is a schema that at least five live players already ship. The only part nobody owns is the issuer's workflow, so Pholio should become the system an event or publication runs its casting and credits through, not a credit graph.

## 2. Three strongest arguments

**A. The credit record is already taken, and the closest copy has stalled in public.** [FACT]
- **Mosaic** is not new.
  - Its public beta opened on 12 Feb 2024, with credits authenticated by a company person's company email plus "human peer-proofing" ([Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/mosaic-imdb-creators-credit-accreditation-50254/)).
  - It relaunched under the Creators Guild of America in March 2026 with a Creator ID, collaborator-verified credits and 12,000+ registered creators ([Mediaweek](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/safeguard-the-identities-meet-the-imdb-for-creators)).
  - Its live statuses read "Authenticated by / In review by / Issue flagged by [org]". That is our ISSUED tier. It is free and names no buyer-side search ([CGA](https://creatorsguildofamerica.org/credits)).
- **Visie** offers full-team crediting with curated profiles. Creatives pay $0, $10 or $30 a month for "preferred ranking" ([pricing](https://visie.io/pricing)).
- **Instagram** has had role-labeled enhanced tags since 2022, and its collab posts are invite-and-accept. I found no 2025–26 change to either.
- **Models.com** credits top-tier shows, Parsons MFA included. **Behance** has had co-owner credits for 56M members ([R1](research/R1-credit-systems.md), [R4](research/R4-demand-distribution.md)).
- [INFERENCE] Mosaic has had 2.5 years, a guild and org authentication, and it still shows no buyer pull. It is the live test of our thesis, and so far it reads "registrations, no market."

**B. In the credit-graph graveyard, the survivors are the ones where the credit came for free.** [FACT + INFERENCE]
- Jaxsta held 380M issuer-supplied credits and still closed.
- The Dots (£5.5M) turned into white-label software.
- Polywork ($40M+) returned capital, and Read.cv was acqui-hired.
- Clowdy and Credictive are gone ([R1 patterns 1, 2, 10](research/R1-credit-systems.md)).
- The survivors (IMDb, music royalties, union rosters, Models.com uploads) all record credits as a by-product of required work.
- MUTUAL, CLAIMED and Instagram drafting are the chore pattern. Only ISSUED rides on someone else's mandatory work.

**C. The uncontested ground is the issuer's intake desk, and the incumbent just raised prices.** [FACT + BET]
- **Kavyar** is the rail for indie fashion magazine submissions, and it already requires a team credit with Instagram handles on every photo ([R4 §2](research/R4-demand-distribution.md)).
- On 14 Dec 2025 Kavyar removed free publisher accounts ([Kavyar help](https://help.kavyar.com/article/a6pzp4ugix-publisher-plans-are-changing-what-you-need-to-know)):
  - $49 → $99/mo
  - $199 → $499/mo
  - Enterprise from $2,400/mo
- Creatives on Kavyar also pay: PRO is $14 and ELITE is $32 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1510274904)).
- **FWB** registers people on Google Forms, yet it already holds the full HMU, styling, dresser and casting roster for every show.
- R1 found no 2023–26 startup offering organizer-issued event credits.
- [BET] Small issuers will adopt a cheap tool that takes them from intake to a published credit page. For them the credit is the payoff of work they already do.

**Who copies this in a quarter:**
1. **Kavyar** can add "Published in X" credits in weeks.
2. **Models.com** could open a long-tail tier.
3. **Cosmos** ($15M, Jan 2026) runs AI attribution of each image's maker. I found no claim or team feature ([Cultured](https://www.culturedmag.com/article/2026/03/31/art-cosmos-odessa-azion-app/)). It threatens the auto-draft idea, not the issuer desk.
4. **Mosaic** could add event orgs as authenticators.
5. **Meta** has had the capability for four years and hasn't used it: low probability, but fatal if it acts.

## 3. The claim most wrong
**"Paid search seats on the verified graph, on the IMDbPro model."**
- That seat market is already occupied. Models.com sells Pro at $20/mo and caps free accounts at 50 page views a month, on the curated tier that buyers actually search ([R2](research/R2-adjacent-platforms.md)).
- Our graph would cover the tier Models.com deliberately excludes, which is the tier buyers search least. R4 found no hirer asking "is this credit verified?"
- Mosaic hasn't shipped search in 2.5 years.
- IMDbPro rests on a canonical record (screen credits). Fashion has none below Models.com.
- Building a free creative side for a seat business with no evidence is the Dots/Polywork arc, done on purpose.

The runner-up is "drafted from Instagram tags." [ASSUMPTION] It depends on Meta API permissions, Cosmos does it better, and it automates the least trusted tier.

## 4. Proposed revision
**Pholio is the casting and credits desk for creative events and publications. Every show or issue run through it publishes a full-team credit page, and every participant receives that credit on their portfolio automatically.**
- **Issuer product:** open-call intake that replaces Google Forms and Kavyar submissions, plus selection and pick lists, call sheets and consent records (all already built). One click publishes a credit page with every role and no 6-person cap.
- **Creative side:** creatives receive credits and curate nothing. A CLAIMED credit stays private until an issuer backs it. MUTUAL is dropped at launch.
- **Issuers in the first 6 months:** FWB and Fashion Week Queens, BKLYN Fashion Academy, People's Runway, Pratt and FIT BFA shows (their coverage publishes no crew credits), and 5–10 indie magazines pushed off Kavyar's free tier.
- **Kill:** search seats, Instagram drafting, MUTUAL, and creative Pro until issuers exist.
- **Test by end of Q1 2027:** at least 3 issuers besides FWB run real intake through Pholio, and their credit pages get traffic from outside the cast.

## 5. Question for the other delegates
**Growth and founder:** name one issuer besides FWB that would move its intake off Google Forms or Kavyar within 90 days, and what would make it switch. If nobody can, ISSUED is a one-customer feature and Mosaic already owns the rest.
